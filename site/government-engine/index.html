The map

Six planes that do not collapse into one another

Authority, jurisdiction, law, public money, delivery and remedy answer different questions. A connection records a reviewed orientation path, not a live determination.

plane:delivery

Delivery

Which operational bodies and service or data rails carry an authorised function?

Maps departments, public bodies, shared digital products, APIs, open data and official statistics without treating technology as legal authority.

Claims attached to this plane (2)
  • claim:delivery-departments · Government departments and public bodies develop and deliver policy and services; their directory listing does not by itself establish a particular legal power.
  • claim:delivery-rails-not-authority · Identity, messaging, payment, forms, APIs and data discovery can carry service interactions but do not themselves confer legal competence or decision authority.

plane:jurisdiction

Jurisdiction

Which legislature, executive or local system may act for this territory and exact matter?

Keeps the asymmetric devolution settlements and four local-government systems separate; topic resemblance is not a competence test.

Claims attached to this plane (2)

  • claim:jurisdiction-local-four-nations · Local-government structures and functions are nation-specific: an England guide cannot be applied silently to Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland.

plane:law

Law

What kind of instrument carries the rule and what route made it?

Separates Acts, statutory instruments, codes, manuals, standards and adjudication, with legislation-status warnings.

Claims attached to this plane (3)
  • claim:delivery-legislation-status · A legislation.gov.uk record can distinguish original and revised text and signal outstanding effects; a contents link alone does not prove that every provision is in force or fully updated.
  • claim:law-guidance-distinct · Codes, manuals, standards and service guidance describe conduct or practice but are not silently treated as Acts or delegated legislation in this atlas.
  • claim:law-primary · Bills pass through parliamentary stages and require Royal Assent before becoming Acts of Parliament.

plane:mandate

Mandate

How are democratic representation, Commons confidence and executive accountability connected?

Maps institutions and constitutional relations without people, parties, current officeholders or inferred personal power.

Claims attached to this plane (3)
  • claim:authority-election-confidence · A UK general election returns the House of Commons; the ability to command Commons confidence is central to forming and maintaining a government.
  • claim:authority-government-parliament-distinct · Government and Parliament are distinct: government develops and implements policy while Parliament legislates, authorises taxation and spending, and scrutinises government.
  • claim:authority-parliamentary-sovereignty · Parliamentary sovereignty is a constitutional principle under which the UK Parliament is the supreme legal authority and can create or end law.

plane:money

Money

Which authority, forecast, control, fund, settlement and audit rail is being described?

Keeps taxing and spending authority, fiscal analysis, central funds, banking, payment settlement, accounts, audit and scrutiny distinct.

  • claim:money-rtgs-chaps · The Bank of England operates RTGS for settlement in central-bank money and operates CHAPS; these payment-system rails are distinct from the public-spending authority chain.
