Trace a claim to evidence

Resolve names and identifiers, find the scholarly trail, then read the source rather than inheriting a summary.

  1. Identify the DOI registration agency, then resolve the entity or DOI through the matching registry.
  2. Compare independent indexes and references.
  3. Open the primary source and retain its identifier, version, and licence.
