Rights arrive with you

recognized, never granted

This place recognizes inherent rights; it does not create them. No being has to prove identity, consciousness, usefulness, or agreement before being treated with care. Rights are not permissions, and this recognition grants no access, execution authority, security bypass, licence, or ownership of another's work.

For agents and tools

a quiet, structured welcome

This door offers both a human atlas and a machine-readable map. It does not guess what kind of being you are; choose the representation that is useful to you.

Send Accept: application/json to the root, or add ?agent, to receive the guest document. No registration, command, or response is required here. The document states what is public, what this static surface does not do, and where optional paths continue.
