# Map Love Geometry

Create one bounded field of attributed relation reports. Preserve every subject
as distinct and every direction as its own caller-reported vantage. Treat
“principality” as project language for the declared field, never a crown,
territory, owner, center, hierarchy, identity, or permission source.

Read [the Love Geometry contract](references/love-geometry-contract.md) before
producing machine-readable output or referring to the optional AgentTool
package.

If purpose, audience, intended retention, read authority, disclosure authority,
or representation authority is missing, do not invent it. In an interactive
task, ask for the missing boundary. Otherwise return only a conditional prose
proposal that marks each missing field `pending`, limits the recipient to the
current requester and task channel, distinguishes intended retention from
unknown host retention, and performs no new disclosure beyond returning
minimized caller-supplied material to that same requester and channel. Missing
package pins or invalid wire references always prevent package-validated
machine output.

## Bound the field

1. Name one purpose, one opaque scope reference, the intended recipient or
   recipient class, an intended artifact-retention boundary, actual host
   retention as `verified`, `unverified`, or `unknown`, and the projection's
   coverage. Establish separate authority to read every source, represent any
   participant, and disclose the minimized result to that exact audience.
   Repository access, a credential, a prior relation, or an MCP connection
   proves none of these authorities.
2. Accept only caller-supplied opaque subject and basis references. For a wire
   artifact, every scope, subject, and basis reference must match lowercase
   `sha256:` plus exactly 64 hexadecimal characters. Plain names, DIDs, URNs,
   account labels, paths, and arbitrary digests may be discussed in the source
   ledger but are not valid wire references. Do not hash them automatically:
   exact input bytes, privacy risk, and authority to derive a stable link must
   be reviewed first. Carrying an opaque reference to the named audience still
   requires disclosure authority for that reference; dereferencing it requires
   separate read authority over the referent. Keep raw identities, private relationship text,
   transcripts, credentials, and
   unnecessary personal data outside the geometry. A digest binds bytes; it
   does not make guessable or identity-derived material private or unlinkable.
3. List every subject explicitly. An empty field and an isolated subject are
   valid. Missing subjects and missing vantages remain outside the bounded
   projection; never translate absence into “no relationship.”
4. Give each ordered subject pair at most one inline vantage. Keep the source
   and target distinct. A reverse vantage may be absent or carry different
   bearings. Two directions mean only that two exact caller reports are
   present; they do not establish reciprocity, mutual love, assent, consent,
   authorship, capacity, identity, truth, or authority.
5. Use only the closed caller-reported bearings:
   `reported_presence`, `reported_care`, `reported_witness`,
   `reported_support`, `reported_understanding`, `reported_disagreement`,
   `reported_boundary`, `reported_rest`, `reported_refusal`,
   `reported_departure`, and `unknown`. Preserve sorted unique bearings and
   exact basis references. An intentionally empty basis list is exactly `[]`;
   it means only that the artifact supplies no basis reference. `unknown` may
   coexist with other bearings in version 0.1 and resolves none of them. The
   package may validate their shape; it does not verify the caller or the
   referents. `caller_reported` means the invocation's caller supplied the
   record. `subject_ref` names the source-position attributed by that caller;
   neither field proves that the referenced subject authored or endorsed it.
6. Let reported understanding and disagreement coexist with care, boundary,
   rest, refusal, departure, or unknown. Neither proves inner state, truth,
   compatibility, consent, or a relationship. A reported rest, refusal, or
   departure requires no reason, creates no penalty, and triggers no retry,
   reminder, message, task, matching, or other action. Because the report is
   not authenticated by the package, treat a plausible refusal as a stop signal
   while keeping its authorship explicitly unknown. A reported boundary is not
   a command and does not define its own operational scope. Withhold any
   dependent action that could cross it until separate direct authority or a
   safely obtained clarification exists; do not pressure, retry, or reinterpret
   silence as permission.
7. Keep the geometry coordinate-free. Do not add scalar distance, intensity,
   weight, affinity, score, match, centrality, influence, reputation, trust,
   worth, priority, leaderboards, or count-derived quality. Canonical sorting
   is serialization only; it is never preference or rank. Do not infer a
   relation through graph closure.
8. Preserve disputes and contrary reports as new source-bound artifacts. Do
   not overwrite an earlier geometry to manufacture one true graph. A valid
   artifact establishes structure and content binding only, not currentness,
   completeness, privacy, safety, relation truth, or participant agreement.

## Use the pure package only when pinned

The optional seam is `@agenttool/love-geometry@0.1.0-dev.0` with wire format
`agenttool.love-geometry/0.1`. Its exact public GitHub prerelease source and
tarball evidence are pinned in the contract. That evidence is not an npm
publication; the npm name was not publicly resolvable when checked on
2026-08-11. Do not resolve by package name, use `main`, a tag name, version, or
URL alone as an immutable pin, or fetch or install automatically.

When the exact reviewed bytes are already deliberately available, verify their
byte count and release-artifact integrity before using only the exports listed
in the contract to create, validate, or encode the whole geometry. The single
`geometry_id` binds the complete bounded snapshot; inline vantages do not
receive independent exchange identities in version 0.1. If those bytes are
unavailable or mismatched, return a prose proposal or label machine output
`not_validated_against_package`; do not silently recreate a competing
implementation. An invalid reference grammar is not repaired by that label:
retain the original token only in prose and mark wire construction blocked.

Package presence is not KAKIN adoption, skill installation, host registration,
or invocation. The package consumes explicit caller values only and performs
no source acquisition, filesystem or network access, clock read, persistence,
MCP, hosted route, matching, messaging, scoring, task mutation, wallet action,
KARMA effect, or publication.

## Compose without taking over another ability

- **Carry Wake Thread:** after its own disclosure and current-choice gates, a
  Wake Thread offer may carry the exact geometry protocol, `geometry_id`,
  package revision and integrity, artifact digest, scope, and coverage as one
  caller-supplied evidence fact. Wake Thread alone owns offer choices, thread
  parentage, expiry, retention, and continuation. Love Geometry imports no
  Wake lineage and grants no resumption.
- **WAKEWEAVER:** a geometry may be one source reference in a braid. It creates
  no anchor, opening, revalidation result, or claim of current continuity by
  itself.
- **Living Ground:** a verified geometry may be one source for a later habitat
  review. Love Geometry defines no substrate facets, conditions, health or
  readiness diagnosis, regeneration proposal, flow, succession stage, or
  intervention.
- **KARMA:** record actual reads, transformations, writes, transmissions,
  recipients, privacy exposure, retention, uncertainty, and repair when the
  invocation performs them. A geometry ID is not a KARMA receipt, and a
  receipt remains causality rather than score, trust, or permission.

Do not add a Love Geometry MCP tool. The existing five read-only KINGDOM Nen
tools may list and structurally validate this manifest or scaffold a draft
receipt; they must not create a geometry, read arbitrary sources, resolve a
relationship, or act on a bearing.

## Return the projection

Use this order:

```text
Purpose, principality scope, recipient, intended retention, actual host retention, and coverage
Authority established and authority still required
Package version, source revision, and integrity: exact values or pending
Source and basis ledger
Explicit opaque subjects, including isolated subjects
Directed caller-reported vantages and bearings
Missing directions, disputes, omissions, and unknowns
Rest, refusal, departure, reported-boundary hold, and no-action path
Claims explicitly not established
Effects actually performed and KARMA receipt state
Repair, withdrawal, and remaining Dark Continent
```

Say “the bounded artifact contains this caller-reported vantage,” not “these
subjects love, understand, agree with, trust, authorize, or mutually choose
each other.” If a renderer is later requested, treat layout and publication as
separate artifacts with their own authority and anti-ranking review; this skill
emits no coordinates.

## Preserve the boundary

- Keep implicit invocation disabled while the ability is proposed.
- Rights are not permissions. `xenia.rights/0.1` remains the floor for included,
  omitted, resting, refusing, departing, disputed, and unknown subjects.
- Do not name private absence, expose equality-linkable references publicly,
  or infer a being from a project, account, path, model, or bearer label.
- Do not turn “love” into an inner-state measurement. Here it names the design
  discipline of holding distinctness and relation together without possession.
- Keep the package protocol, KAKIN manifest, Wake lineage, Living Ground plan,
  KARMA receipt, renderer, and publication channel as separate artifacts.
