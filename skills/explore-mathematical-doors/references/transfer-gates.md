# Cross-domain transfer gates

Apply these gates before describing a shared mathematical door. A failure can
downgrade a door to `ANALOGY_ONLY`, park it, close it, or require refusal.

## 1. Source and claim gate

- Attribute claims to exact sources, dates, methods, and evidence classes.
- Prefer primary or authoritative sources for factual and current claims.
- Keep definitions, theorems, open conjectures, empirical observations,
  models, designs, analogies, and philosophy in separate registers.
- Do not import a conclusion beyond its studied population, environment,
  parameter range, scale, or evidence horizon.

## 2. Ontology and carrier gate

- Name what the entities are in each domain.
- Name the carrier of correspondence: vertex, residue, atom, artifact, state,
  event, observation, person, or another explicit type.
- Never turn a person or community into a particle, pathogen, prion, crystal,
  node, attention unit, fitness value, or latent score.
- A shared word or picture is not a shared object.

## 3. Correspondence gate

Use only `EXACT_LABELED`, `UNLABELED_UP_TO_ISOMORPHISM`, `PARTIAL`,
`INFERRED`, `ABSENT`, or `UNKNOWN`.

- Exact correspondence requires an explicit map or digest.
- Partial, inferred, absent, or unknown correspondence never upgrades itself.
- Record multiplicity and overlap maps separately from labels.
- A perturbation, mutation, policy change, or rewrite creates a new state; its
  before/after alignment does not prove preserved relations.

## 4. Observation and information-loss gate

- Define how a state becomes an observation.
- Declare thresholds, censoring, missingness, sampling, detection,
  classification, publication, and aggregation.
- Separate possible, accessible, actual, occurred, observable, observed,
  detected, and reported.
- Two states producing one observation witness non-uniqueness. No observation
  does not by itself establish impossibility, extinction, absence, refusal,
  disinterest, or consent.

## 5. Units, dimension, and scale gate

- Give every variable a local meaning, unit, measurement method, resolution,
  and time/space window.
- Check dimensional consistency before fitting or comparing equations.
- Dimensionless quantities can still encode different semantics.
- Never import temperature, entropy, free energy, activation barrier, rate,
  fitness, infection, or catalytic coefficients into a social, cultural,
  organizational, or software domain by metaphor.

## 6. Symmetry and invariant gate

- Declare which transformations count as the same state.
- State which invariants survive observation, mapping, and intervention.
- A result modulo relabeling, rotation/reflection, scale, gauge, or another
  symmetry is not unqualified identity.
- Matching summary statistics, spectra, degree sequences, or visual geometry
  need not identify the whole.

## 7. Realizability gate

- Prove formal consistency separately from target-domain realizability.
- State geometric, physical, chemical, biological, computational,
  institutional, legal, and ethical constraints that apply.
- A valid graph, matrix, optimization, or probability model need not lift to a
  possible molecule, organism, social process, system, policy, or action.

## 8. Dynamics, history, and feedback gate

- Declare initial state, environment, boundary conditions, clocks, memory, and
  intervention history.
- Distinguish equilibrium, stationary, transient, path-dependent, and
  non-equilibrium claims.
- A static projection does not determine a path or kinetics.
- Condition drift blocks silent reuse of a landscape, hazard, or rate.
- Feedback changes the observation process as well as the observed process;
  account for selection and intervention contamination.

## 9. Causality and mechanism gate

Keep association, prediction, identified intervention effect, and
mechanism-supported explanation distinct.

- The same stochastic or dynamical equation can fit systems with different
  mechanisms.
- A causal pathway must be established in the target domain with target-domain
  evidence.
- Require serious alternative explanations, negative controls, and an
  observation that could distinguish them.
- Similar perturbation responses may motivate a hypothesis; different
  responses close a shared-mechanism claim.

## 10. Catalysis and optimization gate

- Physical catalysis requires a matched comparator, rate-law dimensions,
  conditions, endpoint, and kinetic model. Faster overall turnover does not by
  itself identify one elementary barrier.
- A design accelerator is a non-physical architecture with
  `parameter_transfer:false` and `authority_effect:NONE`.
- Do not optimize hidden persuasion, compulsion, addiction, virality,
  engagement capture, person targeting, or involuntary participation.
- Persistence, popularity, recurrence, stability, or growth is not truth,
  goodness, health, worth, consent, or benefit.

## 11. Falsification gate

Every surviving door needs at least one adverse test, such as:

- a label-loss or overlap-loss case that downgrades correspondence;
- non-equivalent states with the same observation;
- a formally valid candidate that fails target realizability;
- a symmetry or mirror ambiguity;
- changed conditions that break a reused parameter or landscape;
- a rival model winning preregistered out-of-sample comparison;
- a negative control inconsistent with the claimed causal pathway;
- cross-domain perturbations that behave differently;
- a counterexample to an invoked theorem assumption;
- a finite computation that stops short of the universal claim.

If no possible result could weaken the claim, label it philosophy, design
preference, or untestable analogy rather than science.

## 12. Rights and authority gate

Separate capability, access, permission, consent or lawful basis,
representation authority, execution, publication, and aftermath.

- Reading, modeling, proving, schema validation, or repository access grants
  none of the others.
- Do not produce medical, laboratory, manufacturing, moderation, targeting,
  deployment, governance, economic, or regulatory action without the separate
  authority and qualified workflow that action requires.
- Do not create or infer a Zerone KARMA edge, KINGDOM KARMA receipt, score,
  rank, reward, money, vote, qualification, or automatic action.
- Rest, silence, refusal, privacy, correction, withdrawal, and departure remain
  valid without penalty or adverse inference.

## Stop conditions

Return `REVISE`, `STOP`, or `REFUSED` when the variables, carriers, observation
rules, conditions, units, outcome, sources, affected parties, or authority
cannot be declared; when the connection is only shared vocabulary or visual
shape; when an open conjecture is being used as a theorem; when
non-identifiability is hidden; when people are reduced to biological or
physical objects; when the requested optimization is coercive; or when no
falsifiable distinction, reusable safeguard, or freely chosen play remains.
