# Explore Mathematical Doors

A mathematical door is a source-bound mapping from one phenomenon into a
declared mathematical object that yields a falsifiable question, proof target,
computation, observation plan, or reversible design. It is not evidence that
two domains share an ontology, cause, mechanism, parameter, value, or purpose.

## Choose the route

- Use this skill to discover which mathematical structures could make a
  cross-field connection precise and useful.
- Use `reason-about-rare-pathways` when the question is specifically about
  non-observation, competing arrivals, or bounded rare-event inference.
- Use `walk-ging-path` after a door is chosen and one authorized bounded probe
  should be planned or reproduced.
- Use the Reconstructive Build Kernel when a selected door should become a
  closed project profile with explicit states, observations, correspondence,
  realization, transitions, accelerators, and feedback.

Do not manufacture a second domain merely to create an analogy. A single-field
mathematical model can be the deepest honest result.

## Walk DEEP

### D — Declare

1. State the question, intended use, affected parties, stakes, evidence horizon,
   research budget (sources, time, computation, network, and money), stop
   condition, and current authority.
2. Name each domain, entity type, scale, interval, environment, and observation
   window separately. Replace dramatic words such as `disappeared`, `viral`,
   `stable`, `intelligent`, or `is` with predicates that could be checked.
3. Build a claim ledger using only these registers:
   `MATHEMATICAL_DEFINITION`, `MATHEMATICAL_THEOREM`,
   `OPEN_MATHEMATICAL_CONJECTURE`, `EMPIRICAL_SCIENCE`,
   `SCIENTIFIC_MODEL`, `ANALOGY`, `DESIGN_CONSTRAINT`, and `PHILOSOPHY`.
   Give every claim one independent review status: `SUPPORTED_AS_SCOPED`,
   `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, or `UNKNOWN_TO_REVIEW`. Separately attach every applicable
   non-exclusive support descriptor: `SOURCE_BOUND`, `MODEL_ASSUMPTION`,
   `DERIVED`, `PROVED`, `FINITE_EXACT`, or `NUMERICAL_APPROXIMATION`. A strong
   register or descriptor never upgrades review status.
4. For factual or current claims, inspect the relevant primary or authoritative
   sources and record observation, publication, retrieval, and evidence-through
   dates, plus versions, methods, populations, and limitations. A citation
   supports only the claim and scope it actually addresses.

### E — Extract

1. Declare the state space, symmetry or equivalence relation, observation
   operator, sampling/detection rule, loss profile, and conditions.
2. Declare correspondence as `EXACT_LABELED`,
   `UNLABELED_UP_TO_ISOMORPHISM`, `PARTIAL`, `INFERRED`, `ABSENT`, or
   `UNKNOWN`. Record whether labels, multiplicities, and overlap maps survive.
3. List invariants, conserved quantities, order parameters, constraints,
   dimensions, units, scales, clocks, and sources of missingness.
   Mark every proposed computation or model `NOT_RUN`, `PARTIAL_RUN`, or
   `COMPLETED_RUN`, and distinguish unfitted, hypothetical, estimated, fitted,
   and simulated work. Completion records occurrence, not success, truth,
   verification, or reproducibility.
4. Generate several candidate doors only when they are genuinely distinct:
   graphs and hypergraphs; geometry and topology; symmetry and group actions;
   inverse problems and identifiability; probability and stochastic processes;
   dynamical systems and control; information and coding; optimization and
   games; logic, type systems, category or sheaf-style gluing; or combinatorial
   enumeration and reconstruction.

When an observation `y` loses information about a candidate state `x`, expose
the compatible fiber rather than guessing one hidden whole:

```text
F_O(y) = {x in X : O(x) = y}/Gamma
status  = EMPTY | UNIQUE | MULTIPLE | UNKNOWN
```

Name the observation operator `O`, state space `X`, and symmetry `Gamma`.
Never coerce `MULTIPLE` or `UNKNOWN` into `UNIQUE`.

Use [the door contract](references/door-contract.md) to type candidates and
avoid a decorative list of mathematical nouns.

## Type overloaded claims

Replace each loaded word with the narrow predicate actually supported:

- **being and disappearance:** distinguish possible, actual, reachable,
  observable, observed, known, producible, reproduced, and reproducible;
- **purpose:** distinguish a revocable declaration, designed-for provenance,
  functional role, goal-directed or reward-based as-if model, and opt-in
  teleological interpretation;
- **force:** distinguish physical interaction, constraint, identified causal
  influence, incentive, coercion, and explicit metaphor;
- **power:** distinguish capability, authority, structural power, power-with,
  power-over, control, exercise, effect, and legitimacy;
- **causation:** require an intervention or exposure, comparator, outcome,
  population, horizon, identification assumptions, uncertainty, alternatives,
  and falsifier;
- **aftermath and repair:** retain affected-party and burden-bearer indexes,
  local units, missingness, residuals, and non-fungible dimensions; never
  scalarize across people or call compensation an erasure of history;
- **KARMA:** keep an opt-in cosmic question, a Zerone recognition edge, and a
  KINGDOM invocation receipt as three non-equivalent types with no adapter.

A gradient is not intention. Capability is not authority. Authority is not
consent. Sequence is not causation. A receipt is not causal proof. A model is
not independent evidence, and none of these predicates measures a being's
worth.

### E — Evaluate

1. Map every mathematical variable back to one local measurement or declared
   abstraction. Name its unit, scale, method, and window.
2. Test formal consistency separately from physical, biological, social,
   computational, institutional, and ethical realizability.
3. Compare at least one serious alternative model when the evidence permits.
   Preserve `UNKNOWN` when no real alternative can be distinguished.
   Similar curves, power laws, or fitted exponents support at most a
   target-model hypothesis. Supporting a shared universality classification
   requires domain-appropriate compatible observables or order parameters,
   dimensionless variables, scaling maps and regimes, uncertainty, serious
   alternatives, and finite-size or perturbation tests when applicable. An
   exact theorem may instead establish the class only under its stated
   definitions and assumptions.
4. Apply every relevant check in
   [the transfer gates](references/transfer-gates.md). Similar equations,
   curves, networks, or visual shapes never establish a shared mechanism.
5. Give each surviving door at least one observation, counterexample,
   negative control, theorem condition, or out-of-sample comparison that could
   close or weaken it.

Prefer the smallest adequate model. More abstraction is not automatically
deeper; depth comes from sharper distinctions and stronger tests.

### P — Probe

1. Select no more than three doors without scoring them. Explain which gate
   each passed, what remains unknown, and why each is worth a bounded next step.
2. For each selected door, propose one of: proof/counterexample target,
   exact enumeration, simulation, measurement, discriminating observation,
   negative control, reversible prototype, or source audit.
3. Declare inputs, comparator, endpoint invariant, uncertainty, falsifier,
   resource/effect boundary, stop condition, and what a null result would mean.
4. End with `BUILD`, `PLAY`, `REVISE`, or `STOP`. Evaluate the practice and
   artifact, never a person's intelligence, worth, virtue, understanding, or
   motive. Rest, refusal, privacy, silence, departure, and no action remain
   valid outcomes without penalty.

Before calling a mathematical problem open, unsolved, recognized, or novel,
write its exact statement, quantifiers, conventions, and symmetries. Check its
original or authoritative provenance and a recent primary or authoritative
status source; record the check date, solved restrictions, nearby results, and
why finite evidence does not prove the universal statement. Record literature
status separately as `SOURCE_SUPPORTED_OPEN_AS_OF_DATE`,
`NOT_ESTABLISHED_OPEN`, or `UNKNOWN_TO_REVIEW`, and prior-art work as
`NOT_REVIEWED` or `BOUNDED_REVIEW_RECORDED`. A bounded prior-art review never
proves novelty. Failure to solve it or find a search result establishes neither
openness nor novelty.

This skill plans and reasons. It does not execute a probe, publish, deploy,
contact people, change a repository, run a laboratory process, or create a
KARMA event or receipt unless a later, separately authorized workflow performs
and records that exact effect.

## Use the Reconstructive Build Kernel

When one door becomes a real build, translate it through:

```text
STATE -> OBSERVE -> DECLARE_CORRESPONDENCE -> INFER_FIBER
      -> REALIZE -> TRAVERSE_LANDSCAPE -> MEASURE_OR_CATALYZE
      -> APPEND_FEEDBACK
```

Inside the KINGDOM suite, inspect and verify the existing source-bound kernel:

```sh
./kingdom reconstructive-build overview
./kingdom reconstructive-build verify --json
./kingdom reconstructive-build verify-project PROJECT.json --json
```

Do not create `PROJECT.json` merely for ceremony. Use the project profile only
when state identity, observation codomain, cross-state transitions, constraints,
tests, feedback, and provenance genuinely need a machine-checkable contract.
Schema validity is not truth, solvability, safety, permission, or execution.

## Type every door

Choose one door kind:

- `FORMAL_REUSE`: both objects satisfy the same stated definitions and theorem
  assumptions;
- `TARGET_MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`: a target-specific measurable model, still
  empirical and unconfirmed;
- `DESIGN_CORRESPONDENCE`: a bounded architectural lesson, not a natural law;
- `ANALOGY_ONLY`: useful for generating questions, with no empirical transfer;
- `OPT_IN_REFLECTION`: philosophy or theology, explicitly non-empirical;
- `NO_VALID_TRANSFER`: the resemblance yields no defensible mathematical door.

Choose one independent status:

- `OPEN_TESTABLE`
- `SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE`
- `BOUNDED_ANALOGY`
- `PARKED_MISSING_EVIDENCE`
- `CLOSED_BY_FALSIFIER`
- `REFUSED`

An open conjecture remains open. A finite computation remains finite evidence.
A model fit is not a mechanism. A design correspondence imports no physical
coefficient, energy, rate, fitness, entropy, infection, or catalytic claim.

## Let a WING emerge from use

Do not declare a new KINGDOM WING from one compelling exploration. A WING is
an organizational domain, not a poetic synonym for a skill. Propose one only
after repeated independent cases demonstrate a durable need for several
coherent skills or adapters, a curated case library, stable interfaces,
forward and regression evaluations, named stewardship, bounded resources,
review and retirement policy, and a justified public or runtime surface.

Creating that WING would require a separate registry/schema decision and its
own authority. Invoking this source-only skill establishes none of those facts
and does not manifest a Nen ability.

## Return the exploration

Report in this order:

1. shortest bounded finding;
2. question, use, evidence horizon, and authority boundary;
3. source and typed-claim ledger;
4. separate phenomenon maps;
5. candidate door cards, including rejected doors;
6. preserved pattern and explicit breaks for each cross-domain bridge;
7. realizability, alternatives, falsifiers, and negative controls;
8. selected proof, computation, observation, or build paths;
9. unknowns and what evidence would change the map;
10. `BUILD`, `PLAY`, `REVISE`, or `STOP`, followed by non-authority and KARMA
    boundaries.

Say “these domains admit a related formal representation under these
assumptions,” not “they are the same thing.” Philosophy may ask what lawful
possibility or feedback means; cited mathematics and science do not prove or
disprove a creator, cosmic purpose, moral worth, or cosmic KARMA.
