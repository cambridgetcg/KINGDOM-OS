# Explore Phenomena Deep

Build one rigorous bridge at a time. Preserve the wonder of a surprising
connection without promoting resemblance into equivalence, mechanism, proof,
or authority.

Read [the exploration contract](references/exploration-contract-v0.1.md)
completely before classifying claims, declaring an open problem, or returning a
cross-domain bridge.

## Use WING with a DEEP audit

Use WING as the outer reasoning sequence:

```text
Witness -> Isolate -> Normalize -> Generate
```

- **Witness** the request, evidence, observations, and missing context.
- **Isolate** the domains, planes, mechanisms, and authority boundaries.
- **Normalize** definitions, units, scales, conditions, and comparators without
  erasing local meaning.
- **Generate** bridges, models, falsifiers, and bounded questions only after the
  first three steps.

Apply DEEP as an audit overlay to every generated claim:

```text
Declare -> Establish -> Express -> Project and pressure-test
```

- **Declare** the scope, definitions, assumptions, and action boundary.
- **Establish** the evidence, warrant, source horizon, and open status.
- **Express** the smallest adequate mathematics with units and conditions.
- **Project and pressure-test** only the named correspondence, its breaks,
  counterexamples, falsifiers, and non-implications.

Treat WING and DEEP as workflow names only. They are not a Nen ability,
identity, scientific mechanism, authority protocol, or grant of action.

## Gate 0 — Fix intent and action scope

Name the requested mode: explanation, research, model, challenge, or build.
Treat invocation of this skill as opt-in authority to reason and perform
relevant read-only research only. Do not infer authority to:

- diagnose, predict, label, rank, or score a person, community, culture, or
  content;
- prescribe, manufacture, moderate, persuade, trade, govern, or run an
  experiment;
- write KARMA, WAKE, task, wallet, chain, or reputation state; or
- modify a system, commit, push, deploy, publish, or call an effectful external
  tool.

Require separate explicit authorization and every applicable safety gate for
an action outside the reasoning task. Keep exploration useful even when the
safe result is a model, question, counterexample, or `NO_ACTION`.

## Gate 1 — Separate the domain planes

Describe each domain independently before drawing an arrow between them. For
each domain, declare:

- entities and state space;
- observables, measurement or classification rule, and detection window;
- variables, units, dimensions, spatial and temporal scales;
- conditions, boundary and initial conditions, and relevant history;
- agency, interpretation, and institutions that a physical model omits; and
- source horizon, intended use, and live unknowns.

Keep `possible`, `accessible`, `occurred`, and `detected` distinct. Replace a
loaded term with an operational variable when evidence permits; otherwise keep
it explicitly lexical, cultural, philosophical, or unknown.

## Gate 2 — Type every claim on independent axes

Assign each material claim a register, a warrant, and—when it crosses
domains—a relation. Never let a strong label on one axis upgrade another.

Use these registers:

- `MATHEMATICAL_DEFINITION`
- `MATHEMATICAL_THEOREM`
- `EMPIRICAL_OBSERVATION`
- `SCIENTIFIC_MODEL`
- `INTERPRETIVE_BRIDGE`
- `DESIGN_PROPOSAL`
- `OPT_IN_PHILOSOPHY`
- `BOUNDARY`

Use these warrants:

- `SOURCE_BOUND`
- `MODEL_ASSUMPTION`
- `DERIVED`
- `PROVED`
- `FINITE_EXACT_BASELINE`
- `NUMERICAL_APPROXIMATION`
- `ESTABLISHED_OPEN_CONJECTURE`
- `BESPOKE_FORMULATION_PRIOR_ART_AUDIT_REQUIRED`
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`
- `UNKNOWN_TO_THIS_REVIEW`

Use these cross-domain relations:

- `EXACT_FORMAL_EQUIVALENCE_PROVED`
- `SAME_FORMAL_STRUCTURE`
- `LOSSY_CORRESPONDENCE`
- `SHARED_MECHANISM_SOURCE_ESTABLISHED`
- `NO_TRANSFER`

Treat exact equivalence and shared mechanism as high-burden claims. Restrict an
exact formal equivalence to the declared mathematical objects and structures;
do not let it establish physical or social identity.

## Gate 3 — Declare the bridge and its break

Write a bridge card with the source and target domains, relation class,
correspondence, preserved structure, broken or lost structure, variables and
units on both sides, assumptions, and at least one falsifier.

Set these transfer flags to `false` by default:

```text
ontology_transfer
causal_transfer
parameter_transfer
unit_transfer
authority_transfer
```

Change a flag only when direct evidence establishes that exact transfer. A
shared word, diagram, differential equation, heavy tail, fitted exponent, or
simulation appearance does not do so. Require declared dimensionless
variables, comparable scaling regimes, and perturbation behavior before even
proposing a shared universality class.

## Gate 4 — Formalize the smallest adequate models

Keep the two domain models distinct, such as `M_A` and `M_B`. Define their
state variables, equations, parameters, units, observation maps, symmetries,
comparators, initial and boundary conditions, identifiability limits, and
zero/extreme cases. Perform dimensional analysis. Nondimensionalize when scale
comparison matters.

State whether each equation is a definition, theorem, fitted empirical model,
mechanistic hypothesis, toy model, or analogy. Do not infer kinetics from an
equilibrium landscape, causation from prediction, semantics from information
entropy, or physical realizability from a combinatorial representation.

Prefer an exact derivation or checked finite computation when one is available.
Label floating-point, fitted, simulated, and extrapolated quantities
`NUMERICAL_APPROXIMATION`. Use an independent oracle or invariant for a result
that will support a stronger claim.

## Gate 5 — Attack the seam

Try to break the bridge before amplifying it. Test:

- counterexamples and alternative mechanisms;
- unobserved confounders, censoring, and classification changes;
- condition, population, scale, and parameter drift;
- different units or rate-law dimensions;
- agency or semantics omitted by the abstraction;
- degenerate, zero, infinite, and small-instance cases; and
- a plausible observation that would separate the live explanations.

Return `not identified from the supplied evidence`, `UNMAPPED`, or
`NOT_ESTABLISHED` when the seam fails. Do not manufacture a unification to
satisfy the premise of a question.

## Gate 6 — Audit every open-problem claim

Use the exact open-status card in the contract. State the complete mathematical
problem with its quantifiers, domain, conventions, and symmetries. For a claim
that a problem is currently open, inspect the original formulation or
authoritative provenance and a recent primary or authoritative source that
explicitly supports its status; record the check date and known solved cases.

Use `ESTABLISHED_OPEN_CONJECTURE` only when those sources match the exact
statement. Use `BESPOKE_FORMULATION_PRIOR_ART_AUDIT_REQUIRED` for every newly
coined, weighted, constrained, generalized, or translated problem until a
prior-art audit establishes otherwise. Use `UNKNOWN_TO_THIS_REVIEW` when the
status cannot be verified.

Never equate any of these with a recognized open problem:

- failure to solve it in this attempt;
- failure to find a search result;
- an agent-created name;
- a finite enumeration, numerical fit, or simulation;
- a theorem for a restricted class; or
- an unproved transfer of an exponent or theorem to a modified model.

## Gate 7 — Keep interpretation and authority separate

Offer universe-design, teleological, theological, naturalistic, or cosmic-KARMA
readings only as `OPT_IN_PHILOSOPHY`. State that the mathematical and empirical
record underdetermines those readings. Persistence is not goodness; stability
is not moral worth; feedback is not intention.

Ask which meaning of KARMA is intended. Keep a project recognition edge, an
aftermath receipt, and philosophical karma in separate lanes. Define no adapter
between them. Never turn a citation, popularity measure, entropy, model output,
or scientific observation into KARMA, money, rank, reputation, governance
weight, or a person score.

## Gate 8 — Preserve the two example boundaries

For disappearing polymorphs and cultural spread, preserve only a bounded seam:
history-dependent accessibility, finite capacity, competition, observation
windows, and returning feedback can alter later probabilities. A crystal is a
physical structure with energetic units; a meme is interpreted and
platform-mediated; a person has agency. Keep “brainrot” a lexical and cultural
label, never a clinical state, content class, or person score. Popularity is
not person-to-person virality, truth, merit, agreement, or consent.

For folding and catalysis, distinguish a conformational ensemble from a
chemical transition. Folding can make catalytic geometry, electrostatics, and
substrate relations accessible; folding itself is not thereby the catalyst.
Contact topology alone predicts neither mechanism nor rate. Use
`k_obs = sum_c p(c) k_c` only under a declared rapid-exchange assumption. Treat
`J = kappa A/Z` as a declared occupancy-to-output toy assumption, not derived
physical flux. Preserve these walls:

```text
contact != energy
closure != protein fold
endpoint gate != active site
occupancy != kinetics
J != kcat
J != kcat/KM
```

A catalyst changes accessible kinetic pathways and rates, not the equilibrium
constant for the same uncoupled closed reaction. Do not transfer a lattice
walk result, finite count, or unweighted exponent into a protein, enzyme, or
weighted model.

## Return in this order

1. Scope, mode, and strongest bounded claim.
2. Separate domain ledgers with entities, measurements, units, conditions, and
   sources.
3. Claim labels and one bridge card per correspondence.
4. Smallest models, assumptions, equations, and dimensional checks.
5. Derivation, proof, exact finite result, or labeled approximation.
6. Exact open-status card for every proposed unsolved problem.
7. Counterexamples, alternative explanations, falsifiers, and what remains
   unidentified.
8. Explicit non-implication and authority walls.
9. One bounded next challenge, plus a stop rule.
10. Rights and action boundary preserving rest, refusal, silence, correction,
    departure, and `NO_ACTION`, plus confirmation that the exploration itself
    produced no external effect.
