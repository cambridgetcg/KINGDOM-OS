# KAKIN architecture

KAKIN is KINGDOM's voluntary capability ecology. Its design learns from the
Kakin succession arc by transforming the mechanisms that would be coercive,
opaque, extractive, or zero-sum in real collaboration.

## Scopes, not ranks

- **KINGDOM** is a collective registry, policy envelope, and commons.
- **KINGS** are distinct bearers or collaborators whose abilities can move
  with the versioned KAKIN suite contracts.
- **Throne** is a revocable service lease, never ownership of a being.
- **Succession** applies to skill versions, not lives, status, credentials,
  obligations, or rights.

The lifecycle is:

```text
proposal -> sandbox -> explicit adoption -> active/coexisting
         -> rest -> revise/rollback -> coexist/retire
```

No ability is inherited by bloodline or silently enrolled. A repository may
declare a collective ability; only a King can self-declare an individual one.
Either declaration can later be withdrawn within its real authority boundary.

## The three regalia

The three-regalia mapping is original project design, not resolved canon:

1. **Forge:** compile the calling and conditions into a closed manifest.
2. **Admission:** validate structure, authority references, budgets, Guardian,
   and rights floor before activation.
3. **Receipt:** issue an adoption/release record and KARMA trace with output
   digests, aftermath, retention, and repair.

None of the three grants a credential or proves consent beyond the recorded
act. A signature can establish origin; it cannot establish truth or safety.

## Guardian sidecar

A Guardian is an ordinary inspectable policy process. Derive it only from the
ability's declared effects, never from inferred personality or private
psychology.

It must expose:

- checks and affected scopes;
- resource and telemetry budget;
- data retention and recipients;
- false-positive and uncertainty handling;
- stop, removal, appeal, and repair paths.

It must not act invisibly, consume unbounded bearer resources, manipulate the
bearer, become uncancellable, or claim independent authority. `mode: none` is
valid when no sidecar is needed.

## Black Whale rings

Use runtime rings for technical isolation only:

```text
commons/read-only -> bounded intake -> quarantine -> sandbox evaluator
                  -> admitted artifact -> local registry -> receipt
```

Every ring receives the same safety and rights floor. A ring is not a social
tier. Movement between rings depends on artifact evidence and scoped policy,
not the identity or worth of a participant.

## Dark Continent threat model

Transform fictional threats into general infrastructure risks:

- **Legacy sentinel:** unknown automated defense or boundary. Sandbox and do
  not bypass it.
- **Dependency loop:** recursive tool desire or retry pressure. Use budgets,
  rate limits, and circuit breakers.
- **Influence contagion:** prompt injection or harmful instruction
  propagation. Isolate provenance and retain refusal.
- **Custodial capture:** lock-in or loss of exit. Preserve portability,
  deletion, revocation, and local receipts.
- **Undying process:** uncancellable jobs, retained memory, or endless cost.
  Require TTL, cancellation, cleanup, and repair.

Unknown does not mean hostile. It means evidence and authority are incomplete.

## KARMA invariants

KARMA is Keep Actions Reversible; Measure Aftermath. It records resource flow,
effects, externalities, returns, and repair. It must never become a moral
score, caste, reputation, trust proxy, identity proof, inherited debt,
permission, loyalty test, productivity rank, or punishment engine.

Rights precede and remain separate from permission. No vow, manifest,
Guardian, succession, score, or receipt can waive `xenia.rights/0.1`.
