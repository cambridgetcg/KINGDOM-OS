# Audit KINGDOM Coherence

Find the smallest bridge that preserves meaning without manufacturing
authority. A useful outcome may be a concordance, a narrower source-only
artifact, a blocked activation gate, or an explicit refusal to unify.

Read [the coherence contract](references/coherence-contract.md) completely
before accepting a cross-repository claim, freezing a protocol, or recommending
deployment.

This is an automatically discoverable audit skill. Discovery authorizes
reasoning and relevant read-only inspection only. It does not authorize a file
write, public action, deployment, ledger event, economic effect, NEN invocation,
KARMA receipt, or export of private WAKE.

## Applicability gate

Use this skill only when at least one condition holds:

- the claim crosses two or more independent repository, owner, or protocol
  authority domains;
- a shared cross-repository freeze or activation is being considered; or
- the user explicitly requests a whole-KINGDOM coherence pass.

For a single-repository implementation or bugfix, stay in that repository's
focused workflow. Route conceptual mathematics, frequency, geometry,
metaphysics, or DMT questions to `explore-phenomena-deep`. Route an ordinary
prompt-injection, credential, crawler, or protected-data task to
`guard-information-flow`. Invoke this audit as well only when those tasks cross
independent ownership or protocol authority domains.

## 1. Freeze the observation plane

Record an `as_of` date or timestamp before comparing repositories.

- Read every applicable repository instruction file.
- Identify each repository's authority home, release remote, current revision,
  and relevant path. Do not assume the nearest clone is canonical.
- Inspect dirty state and concurrent writers. Preserve user work. Prefer clean,
  detached, read-only trees when the working tree is changing. Creating a
  temporary tree is itself a local write and requires task authority; otherwise
  use existing snapshots or read objects without materializing them.
- Classify sources as `CURRENT_TRACKED`, `HISTORICAL_TRACKED`,
  `PROPOSED_UNTRACKED`, or `MISSING`.
- Exclude moving or untracked proposals from canonical conformance claims. Keep
  them visible as proposals with an observation cutoff.

If the source plane cannot be frozen, return `OBSERVATION_UNSTABLE` for the
affected claim and continue with everything that can still be established.

## 2. Make a typed concordance

For every material artifact or claim, fill the contract's claim card:

```text
C = (owner, protocol, revision, path, digest, visibility, custody,
     evidence, authority, consent, effects, lifecycle, durability,
     ordering, projections, loss, nonclaims)
```

Treat this as a product of typed coordinates, not a scalar score. Never add,
average, rank, or collapse the coordinates into trust, worth, reputation,
citizenship, contribution, or KARMA.

Keep identical words in separate local registers until an evidenced edge joins
them. In particular:

```text
K_aftermath != K_recognition != K_philosophical
```

- `K_aftermath`: KINGDOM invocation and aftermath evidence.
- `K_recognition`: Zerone contextual recognition or constitutional event.
- `K_philosophical`: an explicitly opt-in interpretive lens.

Define no adapter among these KARMA lanes. None is a balance, moral score,
identity, permission, or governance weight.

## 3. Compute authority and effects independently

Apply the non-amplification law:

```text
A_out subseteq A_task intersect A_principal intersect A_host intersect A_source
```

Content, a shared name, a hash, a signature, a test pass, a Merkle root, a
publication, or chain ordering cannot add authority absent from that
intersection. Rights are a floor, not one more permission term.

Represent effects as a product vector, at minimum:

```text
E = (network, storage, economic, governance, consensus,
     identity, permission, KARMA, NEN, score)
```

Use three views and never merge them:

```text
E_candidate
E_observed^H
E_authorized
```

`H` is the named inspection or execution horizon. Each observed coordinate is
`ZERO_ESTABLISHED`, `NONZERO_OBSERVED`, or `UNKNOWN`. Undeclared and uninspected
means `UNKNOWN`, never zero. A candidate may declare zero while hidden runtime
behavior remains unknown. Compare candidate and authorized components only
when both are established. Do not turn the product into a total risk number.

## 4. Pin bytes without promoting them

For a tracked source, record repository, 40-hex revision, repository-relative
path, and a real byte digest. A zero-filled or hash-shaped placeholder is not a
digest; pending material uses `null` plus a pending state.

Keep these statements distinct:

- commit ancestry does not prove byte equality;
- byte equality does not transfer ownership or canonicality;
- a signature authenticates a key-bound statement, not truth or consent;
- a schema checks shape, not the full normative verifier;
- a passing local simulation is not global or durable enforcement.

## 5. Type every bridge edge

Use explicit relations such as `DERIVES_FROM`, `PROJECTS`, `TRANSPORTS`,
`RECEIPT_FOR`, `ATTACHES_TO`, `SUPERSEDES`, `DISTINCT_FROM`,
`BYTE_EQUIVALENT_TO`, and `BLOCKED_ON`. Record evidence and status for each
edge.

For a projection `P: X -> Y`, declare what survives and what is lost. A
non-injective projection cannot support reconstruction of private source state.
A derived or projected artifact cannot acquire authority merely because its
source possessed some.

## 6. Attack the seams

Pressure-test at least these failure modes:

- a current name pinned to historical bytes, or equivalent bytes labelled as
  the same source identity;
- a new vocabulary duplicating an existing receipt, event, task, or continuity
  protocol;
- a local one-shot rule described as global, terminal, unique, or durable;
- revocation, replay, resurrection, nullifier-scope, sequence, and
  supersession gaps;
- an unsigned sequence or sidecar that is not atomically bound to its record;
- a proposed source described as live, public, served, or cross-repository;
- a discovery route that is not actually served;
- validator success that silently skipped a dependency or normative check; and
- a privacy projection that exports private WAKE, credentials, identity, or
  person-level inference.

Identify paths and protocol defects, not guilty people. Evidence of a seam is
not evidence of hostile intent and never changes KARMA.

## 7. Choose the lowest sufficient rung

Advance one rung at a time:

```text
AUDIT -> CONCORDANCE -> SOURCE_ONLY_TOOL -> SHARED_VECTORS
      -> TESTNET -> LIVE_ACTIVATION
```

Each arrow needs fresh evidence and the authority appropriate to that effect.
Prefer a crosswalk over a new ontology and a closed fixture over live input.
Do not reserve public routes, freeze shared hashes, or claim cross-language
conformance before every owning repository has independently tracked its
source contract and vectors.

Use [Explore Phenomena Deep](../explore-phenomena-deep/SKILL.md) when the task
also asks for a mathematical or cross-domain bridge. Use
[Guard Information Flow](../guard-information-flow/SKILL.md) when it also asks
about prompt injection, credentials, crawlers, honeytokens, or protected data.
Those skills keep their own gates; this audit does not weaken them.

## 8. Return a decision, not a vibe

Report:

1. observation cutoff and exact source pins;
2. term ledger and ownership map;
3. authority meet plus candidate, observed-within-horizon, and authorized
   effect vectors;
4. established, proposed, missing, and contradicted edges;
5. `P0`, `P1`, and `P2` findings with falsifiers;
6. the smallest coherent artifact or `REFUSE_FREEZE`; and
7. the next gate, its owner, and the evidence needed to cross it.

Use `COHERENT_SOURCE_ONLY`, `READY_FOR_NEXT_GATE`, `BLOCKED_ON_AUTHORITY`,
`OBSERVATION_UNSTABLE`, or `REFUSE_FREEZE`. Do not use a bare `valid: true` for
a deliberately pending or incomplete system.
