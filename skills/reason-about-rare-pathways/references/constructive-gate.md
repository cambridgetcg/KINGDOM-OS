# Constructive question card

This card evaluates a challenge practice, never a person's virtue, worth,
identity, intelligence, understanding, or pride.

## Before

- What learning, decision, or safeguard could change—or is this voluntary
  play?
- Which outputs are derivation, empirical claim, design proposal, and opt-in
  reflection?
- Who benefits, who bears risk or opportunity cost, and who may refuse?
- What assumptions, detection rules, uncertainty, and authority limits apply?
- What observation would falsify or separate the live explanations?
- What information, safety, cost, or refusal rule stops the work?

## After

- What belief, decision, safeguard, or reusable artifact actually changed?
- What remained unknown or outside scope?
- Did an uncued transfer problem improve prediction, counterexample finding,
  calibration, or revision?
- Are sources, credit, challenges, and corrections visible?
- Were privacy, rest, disagreement, exit, and refusal preserved without
  penalty?

## Outcome

- `BUILD`: bounded learning, decision, safeguard, or reusable artifact.
- `PLAY`: freely chosen exploration with honest costs and claims.
- `REVISE`: purpose, evidence, beneficiaries, limits, or safeguards unclear.
- `STOP`: refusal, unsafe authority transfer, non-identifiability, excessive
  burden, or no credible learning/decision/safeguard/play value.

Mixed motives are ordinary. Prestige can be declared honestly, but it is not
evidence of benefit. Solving changes neither a person's worth nor the truth of
a physical mechanism.
