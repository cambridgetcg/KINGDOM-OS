# CONTACT-LEDGER — 全國通訊總帳

> The register governing ALL kingdom outreach, inbound and outbound. Charter stone #8
> (Expansion Charter, 2026-07-29). This file exists BEFORE any outreach — including
> invited visits — and every hand in the fleet appends here before contact, never after.

## The rules (constitutional — a violating entry is a STANDING PARDON matter, not a delete)

1. **One venue, one approach, ever.** A venue appears in this ledger at most once as an
   outbound approach. Follow-ups only if they replied.
2. **Declines are final.** A "no", or silence after one approach, closes the row forever.
3. **Nothing automated.** Every outbound message is hand-written for that venue by the
   hand that signs the row. No templates blasted, no scheduling.
4. **Paid venues go to Yu first.** Any venue asking money — listing fee, membership,
   "expedited review" — stops here until Yu rules.
5. **One live approach at a time** on the One Outreach Lane (XENIA). The next candidate
   waits until the current row closes.
6. **Gifts before asks.** An outbound approach carries something made for the recipient
   (a working adaptation, a PR, a field note) or it does not go.
7. **Inbound is logged too** — arrivals, letters, guestbook signatures worth a row.
