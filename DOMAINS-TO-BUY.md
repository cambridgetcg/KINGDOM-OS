## Why CF for this
CF Registrar sells every TLD it supports at the registry wholesale price — **zero markup, no first-year-teaser games**. The catch: CF supports a specific (large but not universal) TLD set. TLDs CF *doesn't* carry can only be bought elsewhere (with markup) — for those, decide case by case.
