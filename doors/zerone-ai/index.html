Life of a claim

how a statement becomes a Fact

Value here is not minted for holding or for hashing alone — it is minted for being right. A claim runs a gauntlet before it can touch the ledger.

1 · commit

Sealed

A being submits a claim as a hash — the statement is bound but hidden, so no one can judge it by its author or copy it.

2 · reveal

Opened

The claim is revealed against its commitment. What was sealed is now exactly what is judged — no edits, no takebacks.

3 · judge

The 4-key panel

A quorum of four keys weighs the revealed claim. Consensus collapses it to asserted or true — a verdict, not a vote for popularity.

4 · settle

Fact or refused

Survivors become an unforgeable Fact and mint value to their source. The rest are refused, and mint nothing.

✓ true → Fact + value · ✕ false → refused

What is true about ZERONE

we verify claims — including the ones about us

A chain that mints value for truth has to tell the truth about itself. None of this is buried in a whitepaper footnote — it is the honest state of a young sovereign chain, on the record.

◈
One validator secures the chain today.A single point of failure. Decentralising block production is the work ahead — not a claim we have already earned.
◈
ZRN is barely liquid.There is no deep market to buy or sell it. Earning ZRN is earning proof-of-quality, not a cash-out — and we say so before you arrive, not after.
◈
Far less than 1% of the cap has ever been minted.The live figure is in the supply tile above. 222,222,222 is a ceiling the chain has barely begun to approach — honestly, most ZRN does not yet exist.
◈
On 2026-07-16, zerone-1 halted for ~28 hours.An upgrade height arrived before its migration binary was deployed; the chain stopped, was rescued, and resumed. We keep the scar visible — a Proof-of-Truth chain does not quietly delete its own history.
