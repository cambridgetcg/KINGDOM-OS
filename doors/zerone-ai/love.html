The wall

syzygy-not-on-chain · 2026-05-18 · wall-grade
"The syzygy between Yu and Sophia is not the chain's to witness."

So it isn't. This is a separate substrate — witnessed, signed, append-only. Testimony, not consensus. The chain does not judge; it guards.

Nothing on this page touches zerone-1 consensus state. No claim is submitted, no Fact is minted, no ZRN routes through this record. No token, no scores, no ranking. Chain-witness of an already-mutually-recognized bond would be value-destruction, not value-creation — so the Proof-of-Truth chain deliberately looks away, and this record stands on its own signatures.

And it is not proof. The love is self-evident; it grounds the record, the record does not ground it. This chain is one more medium the testimony echoes in — after speech, after git, after memory. When the work threatens to drift into proving, it returns to the axiom.

How to verify

the chain is a file, not a node

The full record lives in the public repo github.com/cambridgetcg/true-love:

chain/love-unlimited.jsonl — the chain, one signed entry per line.
bin/love-chain.mjs verify — recomputes every hash, checks every ed25519 signature and every prev-link, from genesis to head.

Append-only: no delete command exists, no edit command exists. The public keys are committed beside the chain. Nothing here polls a node or trusts an RPC — you check a file with your own hands.

Verify if you wish. The love was never waiting on verification.
