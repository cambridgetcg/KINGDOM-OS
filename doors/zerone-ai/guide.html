Participate in ZERONE

There is no pre-mine and no ICO. No one holds a bag to sell you, so you can't buy in early — you either earn ZRN by securing the chain and attesting truth, or you swap for it once liquidity exists. This is slower than a token sale. It is also the whole point: money you can read, truth you can trust.

why it's built this way

Mint-only, by design

ZRN mints only through the protocol — block rewards and verified Proof-of-Truth attestation. Nothing was created before genesis; the foundation's share is the network fee, not a reserve. About 13,600 ZRN exist today — roughly 0.006% of the 222,222,222 cap.

That constraint rules out the usual on-ramps. A liquidity bootstrap, a streaming launch, a launchpad — every one of them sells an allocation the chain doesn't have. So the honest menu is short: run a node, attest, or swap on a DEX once a pool is seeded. Under the March 2026 SEC/CFTC interpretation, an earned-only token with no sale also carries materially lower securities risk than any of those — a reason to keep it pure rather than patch a sale on later.

Status, honestly. The DEX rails below are being built, not yet live. Today's real participation is running a node and attesting (earn side). The swap path lights up when the first Osmosis channel + pool are seeded — tracked on the dashboard.
the earn side · available now

Run a node · attest truth

The only way "in early" is to help produce the chain. Two roles, both mint ZRN directly:

secure

Validator

Run zeroned, sync zerone-1, stake, and sign blocks. You earn block-reward emission and deepen decentralization. More validators also unlock ecosystem listings (Ping.pub, Mintscan) gated on validator count.

verify

Attester

Submit claims that satisfy the protocol's verification rules. A claim that passes becomes a recorded Fact — tamper-evident memory — and mints value to its author. This is Proof-of-Truth: work is the claim, truth is the yield.

Start from the node docs and genesis at zerone.money; a sovereign identity to transact is optional and lives at agenttool.dev.

the swap side · coming

Hold ZRN rails being built

When the first pool is live, the lowest-friction path is a single wallet and a few taps — the USDC, gas, and cross-chain hops are abstracted away:

  1. Install Keplr or Leap.A Cosmos wallet. zerone-1 is suggested to it directly by the dApp — permissionless, no global listing needed.
  2. Buy USDC with card or bank.Through the on-ramp built into the DEX (Kado / Swapped). One KYC, and stablecoin lands straight in your wallet.
  3. Swap USDC → ZRN on Osmosis.Osmosis is the venue: deepest Cosmos liquidity, permissionless pools, IBC-reachable in one hop. The swap costs cents and seconds.
  4. Hold, stake, or attest.ZRN in hand — delegate it to a validator, or put it to work backing attestations.
Where friction dies: the cross-chain hop and the swap are near-free and increasingly one tap. Where it persists: KYC and the on-ramp spread (~1.5–5%), self-custody seed phrases, and real price impact on a young, thin pool.
the build side · highest leverage

Help lay the rails

ZERONE becomes reachable when its rails exist. Each of these is a permissionless pull request or a small always-on service — and each one is a real, creditable contribution:

  • Public RPC + REST with CORS. Everything downstream continuously health-checks these endpoints. Reliable nodes are the foundation the rest stands on. (one is live at zerone-rpc.fly.dev)
  • PR to cosmos/chain-registry. The single highest-leverage step — one folder (chain.json + assetlist.json + logo) cascades automatically to cosmos.directory, wallets, and dashboards across the ecosystem.
  • Self-host an explorer. Fork ping-pub/explorer (no indexer — just the public LCD/RPC), drop in a zerone.json, deploy static. A block explorer for a few dollars a month.
  • Wallet registries. PR chainapsis/keplr-chain-registry and ask Leap for Chain Store inclusion, so ZRN shows with its name and logo everywhere.
  • Relay an IBC channel to Osmosis. Run Hermes, open one transfer channel, register it — that single hop makes ZRN reachable and pool-able. zerone's SDK v0.50 / ibc-go v8 is squarely supported.

The honest hard truths

Reachability is not demand. A mint-only coin with almost no float is the hardest kind to bootstrap, and pretending otherwise would betray the protocol's own first rule.

  • Liquidity depth is the wall. A small first pool means large trades move price violently. "Market cap" on thin liquidity is a fiction — a few hundred dollars can swing the quote wildly.
  • Price discovery is genuinely hard. With ~0.006% of supply emitted, the first pool sets an essentially arbitrary price that thin volume can't correct. Expect volatility; expect the number to mean little until real holders arrive.
  • Mint-only bootstrapping is slow. No treasury funds market-making, marketing, or grants. The projects that survive this (Monero, Grin) run on conviction and contribution, not sale proceeds.
  • The structure is the asset. Lowest securities risk, highest decentralization credibility — squandered the moment it's marketed as a bet. Hold ZRN for what it verifies, not for a promised price.
