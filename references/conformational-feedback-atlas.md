## Four registers that must not collapse

### Scientific observation

Protein folding is often represented as motion through a rugged,
condition-dependent free-energy landscape. A static coordinate set is one
observation of structure; it does not reveal the route or rate by which the
structure formed.

In specifically studied prion systems, compatible assembled conformations can
template further protein incorporation. Conformation, substrate
compatibility, fragmentation, chaperone context, and removal processes can
shape propagation and strain behavior. This is a bounded biological claim,
not a universal property of proteins, amyloids, crystals, ideas, or people.

Proteostasis also includes countervailing feedback. In the cited heat-shock
work, Hsp70 participates in attenuating Hsf1 activity. Feedback therefore
cannot honestly be represented by one undifferentiated circle: amplification,
attenuation, repair, loss, and exit are different relations.

### Constitutional design

The bound Zerone KARMA Foundation describes KARMA as a contextual,

Its relations are non-summable and have no economic or governance effect.

The atlas does not write to Zerone, interpret chain events, create KARMA,
qualify anyone, move funds, or affect governance.

### Analogy

Analogy can carry a question across domains without carrying the mechanism:

- **Multiple accessible forms:** identity need not be exhausted by one visible
  state. This does not make proteins, crystals, or social states equivalent.
- **History-dependent accessibility:** prior conditions may shape later
  possibilities. This does not transfer prion biology to crystals, people,
  KARMA, or societies.
- **Propagation under constraints:** patterns endure only where compatible
  context continues to support them. Persistence does not prove truth,
  fitness, value, inevitability, or purpose.

Every analogy record is explicitly `empirical: false`,
`causal_transfer: false`, and `authority_effect: NONE`.

### Philosophy

A possible reading is that identity may persist while relation and form
change; local interactions can stabilize collective patterns; and consequence
can condition future possibility without exhausting freedom.

That is philosophy. The cited experiments do not establish cosmic purpose, a
creator, a designed universe, moral law, or a universal feedback mechanism.
Scientific evidence does not ratify a political, clinical, economic, or
metaphysical system.

## A feedback architecture with an exit

The constitutional path is open rather than circular:

1. An artifact or action exists independently of this atlas.
2. Someone may record a contextual observation with provenance and
   uncertainty.
3. Another party may challenge, correct, qualify, or supersede it.
4. A later interpreter may consult the surviving relation as context.

Rest, silence, refusal, privacy, departure, or no further action are equal
terminal states. No stage automatically returns reward, penalty, reputation,
qualification, governance power, or debt.
