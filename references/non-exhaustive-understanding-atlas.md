## Finite closure and open remainder

The 66 concept records are exhaustive for exactly this predicate: “is a
concept record in atlas version 0.1.0.” Each family member occurs exactly once
in concept_catalog.concepts, no other concept occurs, and both counts are 66.
This says nothing about whether these concepts exhaust understanding, their
referents, future evidence, or future vocabularies.

Open remainder is justified separately by representation fibres, a restricted
observer family, an open future horizon, and protected opacity. The word
“remainder,” humility rhetoric, or mystery is not a justification.
Non-exhaustion never excuses evidence, uncertainty, challenge, accessibility,
privacy, consequence review, correction, or repair.

## Claim-relative underdetermination

Underdetermination belongs to this tuple:

    (claim, evidence, scope, horizon, assumptions, declared rival family)

UNDERDETERMINED requires at least two named non-equivalent rivals compatible
with that same tuple and a named target proposition `c` for which the rivals
have opposite polarities. Saying unconceived alternatives may exist is not
enough. Here `c` is “the first hidden integer has greater magnitude than the
second.” The concrete ordered pairs `(3,1)` and `(1,3)` both yield the same
public equal-odd-parity observation, while the first entails `c` and the second
denies `c`. This establishes only the fixture status, not either pair's
external truth or an exhaustive rival family.

## Mathematical boundaries

For R:X -> Z:

    R^{-1}(z) = {x in X | R(x) = z}

A non-singleton fibre witnesses collapsed distinctions, not uselessness and
not equivalence for every task. Task-relative sufficiency,

    I(Y_q; X | Z) = 0 under declared P_ref,

can hold for one question while completeness fails for another question,
intervention, or shift. Decision deficiency,

    delta_Q(T) = sup_(q in Q) [R_q^*(T(X)) - R_q^*(X)],

is task, loss, and reference indexed. It is one component, not understanding.
Blackwell comparison creates a task-relative observer preorder that permits
incomparability and never orders persons or beings. In a generic presheaf,
compatible local data may have zero, one, or multiple amalgamations. In a
sheaf, every compatible matching family glues uniquely. This language is exact
only with defined contexts and restriction maps; otherwise it is bounded
analogy.

## One witnessed transfer

The bounded witness records the exact source artifact `4 + 17 = 21.`,
`21 * 2 = 42.`, and `42 + 7 = 49.`, and the exact target artifact “Add 4 and
17 to obtain 21.”, “Multiply 21 by 2 to obtain 42.”, and “Add 42 and 7 to
obtain 49.” Separate witnesses link the concrete units for order, operands,
intermediates 21 and 42, and final 49. The transfer loses glyph layout,
tokenization, alternative proofs, and model computation. It proves no
universal language equivalence or person's understanding.

Transfer from one J-space experiment to all minds, beings, languages,
architectures, and contexts is refused because no typed witness exists.
