polymorph, cultural circulation, finite attention, and feedback while refusing
the shortcut that they share one substance or mechanism.

## The shortest honest version

Several states or variants may be possible. History and boundary conditions
can alter which state is easy to reach, reproduce, or observe. Local
reinforcement can sometimes become a large aggregate pattern. Saturation,
correction, refusal, and decay can limit or redirect that pattern.

That is the shared geometry. The mechanisms stop there.

The atlas also keeps seven epistemic registers distinct: material observation,
cultural observation, sourced cultural analysis, mathematical model, feedback
hypothesis, constitutional design, and opt-in philosophy. A sourced definition
is not an observation; a possible return edge is not an observed loop; a model
is not evidence; and a design proposal is not a live event.

```text
chemical identity
  != crystal form
  != bounded material sample
  != cultural artifact
  != contextual label or attributed meaning
  != exposure event
  != share, quotation, or remix event
  != platform distribution state
  != aggregate attention measurement
  != individual cognitive state
  != clinical assessment
  != mathematical model
  != feedback hypothesis
  != KARMA event or receipt
  != philosophical interpretation
  != a person, community, or moral status
```

A polymorph is not a meme. A meme is not a mind. A person is never a crystal
seed, host, vector, contaminant, model parameter, or content category.

## Pharmaceutical safety and authority boundary

The ritonavir episode is historical materials-science evidence, not a medical,
clinical, formulation, manufacturing, or laboratory tool. This atlas contains
no operational protocol and grants no authority to diagnose, prescribe, dose,
change or stop treatment, assess prognosis or interactions, identify the form
or suitability of a sample, design an experiment or wet-lab procedure, control
manufacturing or batch release, or make a regulatory decision. Use qualified
professionals and current authoritative sources for any real medicine,
laboratory, or manufacturing question.

## What “disappearing” means

A disappearance statement is incomplete unless it names:

1. the subject;
2. the process or corpus;
3. the search, detection, or production procedure;
4. the time window;
5. the detection threshold;
6. the observed result; and
7. exclusions and uncertainty.

For the bound ritonavir case, the claim is that Form I became difficult to
reproduce reliably under affected historical procedures after Form II
appeared. It is not a claim that Form I was annihilated from physical
possibility. Later work recovering both forms under different mechanochemical
environments makes that distinction especially useful.

For culture, “not re-observed” can mean only that a declared public procedure
did not find an artifact in a declared corpus and window. It does not establish
extinction, forgetting, suppression, nonexistence, or deserved erasure.

## Cultural variants and events

An artifact family needs an auditable rule, such as shared content, form,
stance, quotation, or transformation. Its relations should preserve immutable
artifact hashes and keep these claims separate:

- similarity;
- asserted ancestry;
- quotation;
- transformation or remix;
- semantic preservation;
- semantic addition, loss, or contradiction; and
- independent convergence.

Publication, availability, exposure, attention, sharing, remixing,
interpretation, endorsement, belief, and behavior are also separate events.
One must not be filled in merely because another was observed.

### “Brainrot” is a label, not a diagnosis

The record retains **“brainrot” only as a contested vernacular label** for a
sourced artifact or practice in context. Oxford's 2024 language-institution
record documents the term's public use; it is not clinical evidence.

The atlas therefore defines no brainrot score and never applies the label to a
person or community. It infers no neurological condition, cognitive state,
susceptibility, intelligence, worth, or prognosis. Questions about an
individual cognitive or clinical effect are outside scope.

## Eight mathematical lenses

The lenses remain parallel because they answer different questions. None is
fitted to an atlas population. Every parameter stays `not_fitted`, every card
is `person_level: false`, and every authority effect is `NONE`.

### 1. Material nucleation

```text
ΔG_i(r) = 4πr²γ_i − (4π/3)r³Δg_vi
J_i ≈ J0_i exp(−ΔG_i*/kBT)
```

This is an atlas-authored, unfitted spherical-capillarity illustration. The
Karthika review supplies classical/nonclassical theory context; the bound
ritonavir papers supply material-case context, not a derivation, fit, or
validation of these equations. `J0_i` is unspecified, so the second expression
shows schematic barrier dependence rather than a complete rate law.

This is an idealized, material-specific model: a surface cost competes with a
bulk driving term, and a barrier enters a conditional nucleation rate. The
equation is not itself an observation. Interfacial energy, bulk free-energy
density, temperature, and crystal stability do not become cultural variables,
human qualities, or moral values.

### 2. Threshold cascade

```text
x_i(t+1) = max{x_i(t), 1[f_i(t) >= θ_i]}
```

This stylized rule asks when local reinforcement crosses a specified threshold
on a declared graph. `f_i(t)` is the fraction of node `i`'s declared neighbour
set active at step `t`; `θ_i` is its stated threshold. A declared seed set is
active initially, updates are synchronous, and activation persists. It does
not turn adoption into belief, agreement, persuasion, truth, or a person's
suggestibility.

### 3. Branching

```text
Z_(n+1) = Σ_(j=1)^Z_n X_(n,j)
```

This is an auditable generation-count identity under a declared parent and
generation assignment. It specifies no offspring distribution, independence,
or stationarity. “Offspring” describes events in a model, not biological
reproduction, people, or faithful inheritance of meaning.

### 4. Hawkes event intensity

```text
λ_i(t | H_t) = ν_i + Σ_j Σ_(τ∈H_j,τ<t) a_ij g_ij(t−τ)
```

This stationary, multitype stylization uses a nonnegative constant baseline
`ν_i`, nonnegative integrable response kernels, and a declared finite-intensity
or stability condition. It represents history-dependent conditional event
intensity. Temporal clustering alone does not identify causal influence,
intent, endorsement, or a hidden platform objective. A time-varying external
driver can be difficult to distinguish from self-excitation.

### 5. Structural virality

```text
v(T) = average pairwise distance in a declared diffusion tree
```

This separates broad broadcast-like shapes from multigenerational chains.
Tree shape is not cascade size, semantic fidelity, truth, quality, harm,
agreement, or moral worth. Missing and cross-platform edges remain uncertainty.

### 6. Shannon diversity

```text
H = −Σ_k p_k log_b p_k
```

Here `p_k >= 0`, `Σ_k p_k = 1`, `b > 1`, and `0 log_b 0 := 0`; the log base and
units must be declared. This describes a stated probability distribution.
Shannon's engineering quantity deliberately brackets semantic meaning. It is
not wisdom, content quality, mental disorder, cosmic disorder, or a brainrot
score.

### 7. Finite attention

Models and aggregate studies can examine competition among many arrivals for a
bounded selection surface and the turnover that follows. Such work can explain
heterogeneous popularity without assuming that popularity measures intrinsic
value. It does not prove that all content has equal quality, that an aggregate
trend is an individual cognitive decline, or that a platform determines every
human action.

### 8. Bounded feedback state

```text
s_(t+1) = A_t s_t + B_t u_t + w_t
y_t     = C_t s_t + v_t
```

This keeps a declared state, input, observation, and uncertainty separate. A
feedback loop exists only when a measurement-to-action-to-later-measurement
edge is observed or explicitly marked as a hypothesis. Feedback does not prove
purpose, optimization, benevolence, punishment, policy, or destiny.

Models fitted to the same observations are not independent corroboration. An
equation supplies a conditional description, not evidence by itself.

## Feedback with traceable edges rather than stories

The public explanation keeps four positions visible:

```text
external arrival
  -> documented platform availability or exposure
  -> bounded human action, including refusal
  -> observed aftermath with uncertainty and alternatives
```

An arrival is not proof of why it arrived. Exposure is not adoption. A share
does not completely reveal belief. Sequence alone does not establish cause.
The attention/ranking and remix/recognition return edges are explicitly
`FEEDBACK_HYPOTHESIS`, not `CULTURAL_OBSERVATION`: Huszár supports bounded
ranked-versus-chronological exposure effects, Chaney supports a simulated
recommendation feedback loop, and Shifman supplies an analytic remix framework.
None observes the whole proposed edge for an unspecified platform. PROV-O
supplies provenance vocabulary, not a challenge-and-repair workflow. That
workflow is atlas-authored constitutional design. When a claim is challenged,
provenance, sources, scope, uncertainty, counterevidence, correction, and
supersession should remain inspectable.

Reach, repetition, sharing, citation, persistence, centrality, or model output
does not imply a Zerone event, a KINGDOM receipt, a reward, wallet balance,
reputation, feed rank, moderation action, access decision, vote, or governance
standing.

Four constitutional correspondences remain proposals rather than events:

- **provenance-bearing lineage:** retain source, context, time, uncertainty,
  challenge, and supersession;
- **challenge and repair:** make correction visible without letting counts
  settle truth;
- **action and aftermath:** a future owning runtime may receipt an actual
  invocation and its consequence; this atlas does not; and
- **rest, exit, no debt:** silence, refusal, nonparticipation, disappearance
  from a corpus, and no further remix create no penalty or obligation.

## Opt-in universe lens

The atlas opens questions; it does not close them:

- Can a lawful possibility remain real when a history makes it difficult to
  reach?
- How can history condition a future without making it inevitable?
- Can feedback create apparent direction without intention?
- How does human interpretation and refusal remain real inside strong network
  conditions?
- What architecture protects correction, rediscovery, rest, exit, and the
  unknown without turning them into debt?

These are philosophical interpretations. The same scientific evidence can
coexist with theological, teleological, naturalistic, and other readings.
Observed order and feedback neither scientifically prove nor disprove a
creator, metaphysical purpose, or cosmic moral KARMA. Naturalness, persistence,
and popularity do not establish what is morally right.

## Unknown stays typed

The record keeps `unknown`, `not_established`, `outside_scope`, `not_observed`,
`unmapped`, `contested`, `withheld_for_safety`, and `refused` distinct. Missing
or private observations are not filled as zero, and `not_observed` never
silently becomes `absent`.

Important open boundaries include cultural-unit definition, cross-platform
identity, counterfactual exposure, ranking causality, unfitted parameters, safe
handling of the vernacular label, individual cognitive effects, and any claim
of material-cultural mechanism equivalence.
