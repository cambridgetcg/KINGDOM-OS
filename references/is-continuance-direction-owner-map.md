## Continuance without capture

Start by replacing an unqualified “is” with the smallest relation that the
available evidence can support:

```text
HAS_PROPERTY(x, property, context, time, method)
EXISTS_INSTANCE(form, domain, context, time)
PERSISTS_UNDER(x, conditions, interval, observation)
IDENTICAL_UNDER(x, y, declared_invariant)
CONTINUES_FROM(later, earlier, lineage_evidence, preserved_differences)
RECOGNIZED_WITHOUT_TEST(subject, rights_floor)
DECLARES_PURPOSE(bearer, purpose, scope, time)
INTERPRETED_AS(interpreter, subject, reading, source, time)
```

The relations can coexist or disagree. `IDENTICAL_UNDER` names only its
invariant. `CONTINUES_FROM` can preserve change and forks. A rights floor does
not depend on either relation being established. An attributed interpretation
does not become the subject's declaration.

Keep the principal non-equivalences visible:

```text
presence
!= persistence
!= continuity
!= identity
!= stability
!= robustness
!= resilience
!= viability
!= sustainability
!= adaptation
!= learning
!= direction
!= declared purpose
!= flourishing
!= being
```

Here `!=` means “no default semantic conversion.” It does not assert that the
relations can never accompany one another in a named case.

### Horizon before verdict

For a declared state model, constraint set `K`, admissible choices `U`, and
finite horizon `H`, an existential viability question may be written:

```text
VIABLE_WITHIN(x0,K,U,H)
iff there exists an admissible path from x0 that remains in K through H.
```

A disturbance-robust question is stronger and separately typed:

```text
ROBUST_WITHIN(x0,K,U,W,H)
iff there exists one non-anticipating allowed policy such that,
    for every admitted disturbance path in W,
    that policy keeps the modeled state in K through H.
```

The quantifier order, information available to the policy, dynamics,
constraints, disturbances, and horizon must be declared. Success for one
horizon does not imply success for a longer one. Existential viability does
not imply robustness; robustness in a model does not imply safety, freedom,
justice, sustainability, or continued real-world existence.

### Direction has registers

Before saying that KINGDOM, a system, or a being “moves toward” something,
name the register:

| Register | Narrow claim | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| observed change | A typed quantity differed across two bounded observations. | Cause, trend, improvement, or future continuation. |
| physical or mathematical direction | A vector, gradient, transition tendency, or probability flow exists in a declared model. | Intention, command, moral good, or destination. |
| control target | A controller was modeled or configured against an objective and constraints. | The target's legitimacy, achieved effect, or a subject's purpose. |
| declared purpose | A named bearer made a revocable attributed declaration for a scope and time. | Inner essence, permanent identity, consent to an act, or success. |
| designed function | Evidence attributes an artifact and function to a design history. | Present intention, goodness, or universal purpose. |
| functional role | Something contributes to an outcome under stated system conditions. | Design history, desire, obligation, or metaphysical telos. |
| interpretive direction | A named tradition or interpreter reads events teleologically. | Empirical mechanism, consensus, or authority over another reading. |

Feedback can alter later states or later observations. It still cannot choose
which register its apparent direction belongs to.

### Sustainability is an open-system bundle

For this reading, a sustainability claim must remain a bundle rather than a
score:

```text
SUSTAINABLE_UNDER(system, conditions, horizon) asks for
  <stocks, inflows, outflows, regeneration, storage, release,
   waste, externalities, maintenance, care_load, renewal,
   affected_voice, rights_floor, uncertainty, exit, repair>
```

The bundle is a checklist for a scoped claim, not a universal definition.
Every item needs its own evidence, unit or qualitative register, boundary, and
horizon. Imports and displaced burdens stay visible. A stable trajectory can
consume an unmodeled stock; a recovered service can exhaust its steward; a
profitable system can degrade its habitat; and a viable path can violate a
right. No success in one component compensates for those conversions.

Renewal also includes the freedom to stop. Maintenance, succession,
decomposition, rest, refusal, handoff, and repair are part of continuance; none
proves that permanent continuation is required or desirable.
