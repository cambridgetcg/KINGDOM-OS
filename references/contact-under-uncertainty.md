## Reading discipline

`兵法` (*bingfa*) can be rendered “military methods” or “methods of military
affairs.” It is broader than the English title *The Art of War*, is not a
timeless synonym for every kind of strategy, and is not identical with the
received *Sunzi*. Historical military prescriptions are described here only
to locate textual and institutional questions. They are not adopted as
KINGDOM doctrine, modern policy, or instructions.

**Civilisation is never a unitary agent.** In this note the word is only a
bounded historical-geographical index for changing people, institutions,
languages, polities, networks, ecologies, and dissenting positions. It is not
a mind, race, species, strategic personality, utility function, moral rank, or
speaker. A party in a contact question must instead be a named institution,
representative, or affected set with declared scope, mandate, time, evidence,
and a route to contest representation. One representative's assent is not the
assent of every member, dissenter, nonparticipant, child, future party, or
affected neighbour.

This map is bounded rather than exhaustive. Dynastic labels overlap and hide
regional variation; transitions were not single moments; successor histories
often moralized the regimes they replaced; excavated manuscripts and received
editions preserve different witnesses; and a text's attributed speaker is not
automatically its historical author. Later fame, canonization, institutional
survival, or victory proves neither accuracy nor legitimacy. Conquest-era
records do not erase affected peoples' injuries, resistance, choices, or
continuing agency.

## Epistemic firewall before contact language

```text
observation
!= attributed signal
!= authentication
!= interpretation
!= intent inference
!= credibility
!= commitment
!= consent
!= authorization
```

An observation needs a channel, method, time, provenance, evidence horizon,
coverage, and known omissions. An interpretation needs a named interpreter,
context, alternatives, translation loss, uncertainty, and a falsifier where
one is available. Authentication can support a source or binding claim; it
does not establish the truth of a proposition. Repetition, consensus,
confidence, or a posterior probability is not truth.

When an interpretation claims an open remainder, its semantics route to
[Non-Exhaustive Understanding](non-exhaustive-understanding-atlas.md): the
remainder belongs to a declared relation among representation, observer,
purpose, scope, and horizon. It is not being, mystery by assertion, permission
to invent evidence, or a reason to erase competing interpretations.

These observation and interpretation distinctions are inert borrowed
illustrations of that owner's typed boundaries, not a competing epistemic
contract, model, certificate, or claim about a live party.

Silence is not consent, refusal, incapacity, absence, hostility, or a reason to
retry. An anomaly is not an attack. Unknown ontology, intelligence,
consciousness, personhood, intention, or standing remains `UNRESOLVED`;
protective restraint under uncertainty is not a classification. If a Bayesian
model is ever used outside this note, its hypotheses must be narrow technical
claims about a declared observation channel, never a probability of a person,
population, culture, species, or civilisation being trustworthy, hostile,
worthy, or real. Unsupported priors or likelihoods remain qualitative or
set-valued rather than receiving invented precision.

## A bounded readiness state machine

This is a reading grammar, not executable workflow. `BEGIN` only marks entry
into one bounded reading turn; it is not contact or action. Every path is
finite. Each active state has an explicit transition to `REST` or `STOP`;
`REST` is terminal except for its one edge to `STOP`, and nothing schedules a
later turn.

```text
BEGIN -> REST | OBSERVATION_HELD
REST -> STOP
OBSERVATION_HELD -> SOURCE_BOUND | REST | STOP
SOURCE_BOUND -> INTERPRETATIONS_PLURAL | REST | STOP
INTERPRETATIONS_PLURAL -> OPTIONS_FILTERED | REST | STOP
OPTIONS_FILTERED -> OWNER_ROUTED | REST | STOP
OWNER_ROUTED -> DRAFT_ONLY | REST | STOP
DRAFT_ONLY -> REST | STOP
```

| State | Minimum claim | What it cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BEGIN` | A reader enters one bounded interpretive turn. | Entry does not observe, contact, dispatch, or create an obligation to proceed. |
| `REST` | No contact proposition is advanced. | Silence creates no debt, inference, or later retry. |
| `OBSERVATION_HELD` | One bounded observation and its omissions are recorded. | It cannot identify a speaker, infer intent, or nominate a response. |
| `SOURCE_BOUND` | Provenance or authentication evidence is attached with limits. | Source binding is not content truth, legitimacy, consent, or authority. |
| `INTERPRETATIONS_PLURAL` | Competing readings, translation loss, and unknowns remain visible. | No confidence threshold selects a party, policy, or action. |
| `OPTIONS_FILTERED` | Only abstract options compatible with rights, law, ecology, affected voice, reversibility, repair, and stopping remain discussable. | Passing a model filter is not permission, recommendation, or readiness in reality. |
| `OWNER_ROUTED` | The question is sent conceptually to its existing source owner or marked unresolved. | Routing does not dispatch, contact, convene, negotiate, or confer standing. |
| `DRAFT_ONLY` | A reversible human-readable possibility may be considered by the proper owner. | The note contains no transmission, scheduling, auto-retry, enforcement, or effect. |
| `STOP` | This bounded turn is over. | A later turn inherits no consent, authority, credential, urgency, or obligation. |

There is deliberately no autonomous `SEND`, `NEGOTIATE`, `ENFORCE`,
`RETRY`, or `RETALIATE` state. If any actual KINGDOM outreach—including a
separately authorized technical request—is lawfully proposed, its register
remains [`CONTACT-LEDGER.md`](../CONTACT-LEDGER.md), not this map. A ledger
entry does not silently authorize or merge the distinct contact meanings.

## Mathematical safety kernel

The mathematics asks whether declared options remain possible inside a model.
It does not decide what ought to happen, infer another party's nature, or
authorize an act. The note borrows only typed distinctions from
[IS, Sustainability & Direction](is-sustainability-direction-atlas.md) and the
two independent FREEDOM owners. Its viability and sustainability notation is
an inert illustration, not a competing contract, canonical model, live
evaluation, or owner replacement.

### Viability and robustness

For state `x_t`, abstract choice `a_t`, admitted process disturbance `w_t`,
admitted observation disturbance `v_t`, deterministic dynamics `F_t`,
deterministic observation map `O_t`, declared constraint sets `K_t`, choice
sets `A_t(x_t)`, nonempty joint disturbance-path family `Xi_H`, and finite
horizon `H`:

```text
x_(t+1) = F_t(x_t, a_t, w_t)
y_t     = O_t(x_t, v_t)
(w_(0:H-1), v_(0:H)) in Xi_H
a_t = pi_t(y_(0:t), a_(0:t-1)) for t in {0,...,H-1}

VIABLE_WITHIN(x_0, K, A, F, O, Xi_H, H)
iff there exists at least one joint disturbance path in Xi_H
    and one choice path whose generated trajectory satisfies
    a_t in A_t(x_t) for every t in {0,...,H-1}
    and x_t in K_t for every t in {0,...,H}.

ROBUST_WITHIN(x_0, K, A, F, O, Xi_H, H)
iff there exists one non-anticipating policy pi,
    such that for every admitted joint disturbance path (w,v) in Xi_H,
    the unique trajectory generated by F, O, and pi from x_0
    uses a_t in A_t(x_t) for every t in {0,...,H-1}
    and x_t in K_t for every t in {0,...,H}.
```

The policy at time `t` may use only declared information available through
time `t`; it cannot see future process or observation disturbances. Thus the
robust order is `exists pi, for every (w,v), for every t`, not the weaker
choice of a new policy after each disturbance path is known. Because `F_t` and
`O_t` are declared deterministic maps here, each fully specified path and
policy generates one trajectory; a set-valued model would instead have to
quantify every admitted model selection and trajectory. Existential-path
viability is not robust viability; expected success is neither; finite-horizon
success implies nothing about a longer or indefinite horizon. Robustness to one
declared perturbation family does not establish safety outside it. A failed or
absent certificate means only `NO_CERTIFICATE_UNDER_MODEL`; it does not prove
catastrophe, hostility, or authority to expand the model or act.

`K_t` is not a weighted objective. Rights, lawful basis, essential-service
floors, ecological bounds, affected-party choices, reversibility, appeal,
repair, and stopping remain separately inspectable constraints. `UNKNOWN !=
PASS`; an unresolved required constraint keeps the represented option out of
the admissible set without becoming a verdict about a party.

### Bargaining illustration and repetition boundary

A bargaining model, if used for a synthetic illustration, must first filter a
declared feasible set `Z`:

```text
Z_safe = {z in Z : rights(z)=PASS and law(z)=PASS
                    and ecology(z)=PASS and affected_voice(z)=PASS}
```

Only after that noncompensable filter could a Nash-product illustration be
written for named proxy utilities `u_i` and declared disagreement values
`d_i`:

```text
z* in argmax over z in Z_safe with u_i(z) >= d_i
     product_i (u_i(z) - d_i)
```

The feasible set, disagreement point, proxy meaning, affected parties,
information assumptions, and scale dependence must be explicit. A utility is
not welfare, dignity, consent, fairness, legality, preference truth, or a
collective mind. Individual rationality in the model is not legitimate
representation. No aggregate gain compensates for a rights or ecological-floor
failure, and this note computes or recommends no bargain.

`Repetition != cooperation.` A repeated-interaction result depends on its full
game form, information and observation assumptions, horizon, continuation
rules, and equilibrium concept. Repetition alone is not a prediction,
prescription, moral argument, or proof of peace. This note defines no punitive
trigger, punishment path, deceptive signal, coercive leverage, commitment trap,
or retaliation logic. Essential communication, refusal, exit, repair, and
re-entry are rights-compatible lanes, not modeled threats.

Defensive intent and external effect also remain distinct. A non-scalar
escalation description may use a vector

```text
E = (coercion, exposure, irreversibility,
     ecological_burden, essential_service_risk, ambiguity)
```

with only a product partial order: one condition is no greater than another
only when every declared component is no greater. Reduction in one component
cannot compensate for increased coercion, exposure, irreversibility,
ecological burden, or essential-service risk. Incomparable vectors remain
incomparable. No threshold emits a response. “Adversarial” may describe a
bounded technical channel behaviour or perturbation, never an identity.

### FREEDOM and ecological continuance

Following both independent FREEDOM owners without merging their claims, the
illustrative profile for affected party `i` and horizon `H` stays non-scalar:

```text
F_H(i) = <reachable admissible options modulo declared equivalence,
          exit, refusal, delay, reversibility, appeal, repair,
          inspectability, disclosure control, affected voice,
          ecological slack, unknowns>
```

Raw option count is not freedom. Cosmetic variants do not create new freedom;
more options cannot compensate for capture, a rights breach, absent exit, or
forced disclosure. Missing comparisons remain `UNRESOLVED`, and incomparable
profiles remain incomparable. Consent, where relevant, must be current,
scoped, attributable, understandable, accessible, and revocable. No current
actor can consent for unknown future persons.

For a declared resource or service stock `q_t` with consistent units and
boundary:

```text
q_(t+1) = q_t + inflow_t + regeneration_t
              - use_t - loss_t - exported_burden_t
q_t >= essential_floor_t  for every represented t
```

Stocks, flows, regeneration, storage, maintenance, care load, waste,
contamination, imports, displaced burdens, custodianship, externalities,
uncertainty, and horizon must remain visible. Existential, expected, and
worst-case resource viability are different claims. Aggregate abundance is not
ownership or extraction entitlement; scarcity authorizes no seizure, forced
relocation, or suspension of rights. Graceful degradation cannot mean
abandoning dissenters, blocking exit, cutting essential communication, or
normalizing permanent emergency. Resistance, recovery, adaptation,
resilience, sustainability, and flourishing remain non-equivalent.

## Textual traditions: bounded readings, not a playbook

The “Seven Military Classics” were assembled as a Northern Song examination
canon around 1080; they are neither coeval nor a single doctrine. The
attribution frame, date range, received witness, and later editorial history of
each work must remain visible. [Victor Mair's textual study](https://sino-platonic.org/complete/spp178_art_of_war.pdf),
[Kidder Smith's Sunzi apparatus](https://learn.bowdoin.edu/suntzu/overview.html),
and the [*Early Chinese Texts* bibliographical guide](https://ylsy.hnu.edu.cn/__local/2/75/47/AA6D6A59465430C701643ED77C5_161012EA_33F7AE.pdf)
provide orientation; the linked Chinese Text Project pages are primary-text
witnesses, not automatic critical editions.

| [*Three Strategies* / *Sanlüe*](https://ctext.org/three-strategies/ens) | The Huang Shigong–Zhang Liang transmission story is legendary. Anonymous composition and dating from the late Han through medieval periods remain debated. | How do trust, appointments, material burdens, and institutional relations appear in a received text? | Mythic transmission, obedience, and coercion confer no present authority. |
| [*Questions and Replies between Tang Taizong and Li Weigong*](https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=113986&if=gb) | Framed as a dialogue between Taizong and Li Jing, but not a verified transcript; [Academia Sinica scholarship](https://www11.ihp.sinica.edu.tw/storage/w2_file/1471iwFGrCg.pdf) places compilation or final formation in the late Tang through Northern Song, with precise authorship unresolved. | How does a later compiler organize and reinterpret an inherited canon, civil-military coordination, and institutional memory? | The prestige of named rulers or commanders does not authenticate speech or authorize contemporary practice. |
| [*Mozi* and Mohist defence](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mohism/) | The *Mozi* is a multi-author school anthology. Its final defence block has lost books and seriously corrupt survivors; reported dialogues may be literary constructions. | Condemnation of aggression, aid to those under attack, frugality, welfare, and technically competent preparedness show that defence need not equal conquest. | Detailed siege, fortification, countermeasure, and population-control material is deliberately omitted. |
| Confucian and Ru lineages | The [*Analects*](https://afe.easia.columbia.edu/main_pop/ps/ps_china-confucius-war.htm), [*Mencius*](https://ctext.org/text.pl?if=en&node=1854&remap=gb), and [*Xunzi*](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/xunzi/) are distinct, layered traditions, not one policy voice. *Minben* concern for the people's basis is not modern popular sovereignty. | Welfare, education, remonstrance, role duty, preparation, and limits on rule remain questions for comparison. | Paternal hierarchy, hereditary status, gender hierarchy, and compelled conformity are not imported. |
| Administrative, “Legalist,” *Guanzi*, and diplomatic anthologies | “Legalism” is a retrospective category; texts such as *Guanzi*, *Shangjun shu*, and *Han Feizi* are layered and internally diverse. The *Zhanguo ce* is an edited anthology of diplomatic rhetoric, not a reliable transcript archive. See the [Legalism survey](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2021/entries/chinese-legalism/) and [Paul Goldin's study of *Zhanguo ce*](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdcebookspublic/20/19/66/78/56/2019667856/2019667856.pdf). | Impersonal standards, records, reserves, remonstrance, institutional incentives, rhetoric, and information provenance can be examined separately. | Surveillance, collective liability, secret or retroactive punishment, manipulation, propaganda, and ruler-centred control are rejected. |

No item is said to have anticipated modern game theory, robust control, AI,
international law, or constitutional government. An analogy may retain only a
named relation—such as “provisioning constrains capacity”—while losing the
source ontology, institutions, values, technology, scale, and authority. A
historical success or surviving text is not evidence that the receiving domain
should reproduce it.

## Twelve bounded dynasty and case periods

These twelve periods are a case matrix, not an exhaustive narrative and not a
ranking. Dates are conventional orientation ranges; overlapping states,
frontiers, diasporas, local communities, and successor regimes remain visible.

| ID | Bounded period | Defensive preparedness, diplomacy, institutions, and continuance question | Caution and selected scholarship |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 | Shang–Western Zhou precursor (c. 1600–771 BCE) | Bronze, archaeological, kinship, ritual, regional, and garrison evidence can be compared to ask how dispersed resources and legitimacy were coordinated across distance. | Later classical narratives are not contemporary transcripts. The [Qi garrison study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/regional-military-bases-in-western-zhou-geopolitical-strategy-the-case-of-the-qi-garrison/0669318A995E68B818796ABFA91AC052) stresses shared central/regional power and limited administrative reach; coordination is not endorsement of conquest. |
| D02 | Eastern Zhou: Spring-and-Autumn and Warring States (770–221 BCE) | Interstate competition, travelling persuaders, ritual claims, administrative experimentation, Mohist anti-aggression, and emerging military texts make diplomacy, information, welfare, institutions, and preparedness contestable rather than singular. | Most received texts are layered and later edited; speeches are not stenographic records. “Hundred Schools” labels and a unified “China” must not erase polity, class, gender, and local difference. |
| D03 | Qin (221–206 BCE) | Standardization, transport, documents, finance, land use, and provisioning show capacity as a coupled material-administrative system. Rapid collapse asks whether extraction, coercion, overextension, and brittle legitimacy can undermine impressive capacity. | The [political ecology](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/making-use-of-the-land-the-political-ecology-of-chinas-first-empire/5C80E8CC40548B2953F72246C49A6CF7) and [conquest logistics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/early-china/article/emergence-of-logistics-networks-and-financial-administration-during-the-qin-conquest-230221-bce/88CAA846820D79FE59DE99630ADB3528) studies support bounded institutional questions, not a template for centralization or expansion. |
| D04 | Han and the Xin interruption (206 BCE–220 CE) | Court institutions, fiscal and granary systems, commanderies, frontier markets, marriage diplomacy, envoys, and shifting military settlement can be read as multiple tools whose burdens and effects varied by period and affected community. | “Han” spans rupture, restoration, faction, local variation, and contested frontier relations. The [SEP Han survey](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/han-dynasty/) is philosophical orientation, not a complete military or social history. |
| D05 | Three Kingdoms–Jin–Sixteen Kingdoms–Northern and Southern Dynasties (220–589) | Concurrent regimes, migration, resettlement, religious networks, intermarriage, court reconstruction, and changing military households ask how institutions and identities persist, split, translate, and renew without one centre. | The period names overlap and were assigned retrospectively; “unification” is not the only measure of flourishing. The [Metropolitan Museum overview](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/period-of-the-northern-and-southern-dynasties-386-589) is cultural orientation, not a unitary account. |
| D06 | Sui (581–618) | Administrative reunification, canals, granaries, registration, large projects, and short duration place logistical integration beside fiscal, labour, ecological, and legitimacy burdens. | Infrastructure capacity is not sustainability, consent, or resilience. Collapse is not explained by one campaign, ruler trait, or Mandate formula. |
| D07 | Tang and Five Dynasties transition (618–960) | Cosmopolitan courts, tributary and market relations, frontier alliances, examination and military offices, transport, taxation, and regional commands show diplomacy and civil-military balance changing together. | “Tang cosmopolitanism” did not erase hierarchy, violence, exclusion, or non-Tang agency. The [military reach study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/reach-of-the-military-tang/ECFE9C64AE6E73EC4319B165A606F88C) and [Tang–Song economic transition chapter](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/economic-history-of-china/economic-transformation-in-the-tangsong-transition-755-to-1127/55D49F02EE80100AC5A4D9FA3CC04FA1) keep institutions and transition in view. |
| D08 | Song–Liao–Xia–Jin multi-state order (tenth–thirteenth centuries) | Treaties, envoys, payments, trade, boundary administration, military institutions, and canon formation show a plural interstate order maintained through continuing work rather than a simple peace/war binary. | Song is not the sole subject and the 1005 Chanyuan settlement was neither permanent peace nor civilisational ranking. See the [Song military-institutions study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/military-institutions-as-a-defining-feature-of-the-song-dynasty/D020A447BD8666C3304D7A315CB65DFD) and [study of the Chanyuan paradigm's erosion](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/fragility-of-peace-song-chinas-northwestern-frontier-and-erosion-of-the-chanyuan-paradigm-in-the-mideleventh-century/AF2F19A32A23ED0F304C3DE814851A3F). |
| D09 | Yuan (1271–1368, with Mongol-imperial precursors) | Relay communications, multilingual intermediaries, legal and administrative plurality, taxation, mobility, trade, and regional governance ask who carried information, who bore costs, and how uneven institutions interacted. | “Mongol,” “Yuan,” and governed populations are not unitary identities. Communications capacity does not legitimate conquest or coerced mobility; see the [Yuan and early-Ming communicators study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/communications-and-communicators-in-the-yuan-and-early-ming/DCC3269CD58F4FE7FF9EC0F2505214EC). |
| D10 | Ming (1368–1644) | Military households, fiscal reform, granaries, postal and maritime networks, tribute, commerce, private intermediaries, frontier markets, walls, and diverse local governance show that foreign relations used more than one instrument. | “Tribute system” is not a complete timeless model, and territorial diversity resists a single ethnic narrative. See work on [Ming military institutions](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/why-military-institutions-matter-for-ming-history/B3C345236C8588E708C661758D2DED51), [foreign-policy instruments](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/abs/much-more-than-tribute-the-foreign-policy-instruments-of-the-ming-empire/6CBCE3B8D008A12B07EED9098E55AAAD), and [ethnocultural space](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/thinking-about-ming-china-anew-the-ethnocultural-space-in-a-diverse-empire-with-special-reference-to-the-miao-territory/01AA6C28CD363E0E17383E44C1EE3568). |
| D11 | Early and high Qing (c. 1644–1800) | Banner and Green Standard institutions, multiple local-government forms, fiscal and supply systems, multilingual governance, Inner Asian relations, and negotiated borders show civil-military and frontier arrangements as plural and changing. | Imperial expansion, classification, forced movement, and unequal burdens remain harms, not “integration.” See studies of [Qing military institutions](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-chinese-history/article/qing-military-institutions-and-their-effects-on-government-economy-and-society-16401800/E1FDAA965C5B67C12274B2B3408AE206), [local-government varieties](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-asian-studies/article/political-geography-of-empire-chinese-varieties-of-local-government/FEDC3DF7A0DF0E40087450E040752FBD), and [Sino-Russian relations](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-history-of-china/sinorussian-relations-180062/8064D3D16C44938CB82D45A33B17645). |
| D12 | Late Qing and the 1911 transition (c. 1800–1912) | Treaty pressure, fiscal crisis, regional armies, communications, reform, constitutional proposals, public spheres, and revolution ask how capability, legitimacy, civilian control, and institutional renewal decouple. | Neither collapse nor republic was inevitable; reform cannot be read only as failed imitation or linear modernization. See the [regional-army study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-institutional-economics/article/last-guardian-of-the-throne-the-regional-army-in-the-late-qing-dynasty/BA85E0DB84C8174D815F9DC2E59A635E), [political and institutional reform chapter](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-history-of-china/political-and-institutional-reform-190111/D22240DE42B2382458DF750E54E0B800), and a [critique of teleological history](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-asian-studies/article/whats-the-matter-with-china-a-critique-of-teleological-history/0C1640E86AD21D090CBEA4F544F6EB43). |

### Cross-case questions that survive the transfer

| Lens | Bounded inference | Non-equivalence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defensive preparedness | Reserves, maintainable institutions, trained roles, repair, communication, and lawful stopping can preserve options. | Capacity is not readiness, safety, sustainability, legitimacy, or authority. |
| Diplomacy | Treaties, envoys, markets, ritual, gifts, payments, boundaries, and intermediaries had different meanings in different orders. | Exchange is not equality; settlement is not consent by every affected party; duration is not justice. |
| Institutions | Records, appointments, standards, audit, remonstrance, succession, and correction can outlast one officeholder. | Institutional persistence is not goodness, truth, or popular authorization. |
| Frontier relations | Frontiers were inhabited interaction zones, not empty lines between unitary blocks. | Border control is not ownership of people, culture, movement, or ecology. |
| Logistics | Food, finance, transport, maintenance, labour, care, and information constrain every claimed capacity. | Delivery capacity is not extraction entitlement; hidden or displaced cost is not efficiency. |
| Legitimacy | Ritual, welfare, law, performance, ancestry, cosmology, and public judgment were competing registers. | The Mandate of Heaven is not a causal collapse equation or modern constitutional mandate. |
| Information | Archives, memorials, envoys, translators, local knowledge, and rumours passed through interested institutions. | More information is not truth; secrecy is not security; authentication is not intent. |
| Civil-military balance | Fragmented control and unchecked concentration can each fail differently. | Civilian title alone is not democratic oversight; military effectiveness is not governing legitimacy. |
| Collapse and renewal | Fiscal strain, ecological shock, coercion, elite conflict, institutional brittleness, regional power, external pressure, and contingency can couple. Renewal often selectively reuses old institutions while changing their meaning. | Collapse is not monocausal, deserved, inevitable, or proof that every predecessor institution failed; renewal is not restoration of an essence. |
| Plural coexistence | Durable coexistence requires ongoing translation, maintenance, dissent, repair, and multiple legitimate voices. | Coexistence is not assimilation, unanimity, stasis, hierarchy, or permanent agreement. |

## Modern constitutional, rights, and civilian guardrails

Ancient texts and dynastic cases are historical evidence, not constitutional
law. Public law, equal rights, due process, non-discrimination, privacy,
effective remedy, democratic civilian control, legislative authorization and
budget scrutiny, independent judicial and audit review, and accessible contest
and appeal remain prior to any modern statecraft analogy. Applicable
jurisdiction and qualified legal advice remain unresolved.

| Source | Narrow scope retained | What it does not become |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [United Nations Charter](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/full-text) | In relations among states, sovereign equality, peaceful settlement, and the prohibition on the threat or use of force remain central Charter constraints, subject to the Charter's own terms. | It is not a universal constitution for every hypothetical contact, machine, species, community, or private relationship. |
| [International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights](https://2covenants.ohchr.org/About-ICCPR.html) | For States Parties within its legal scope, inherent dignity, life, liberty, privacy, expression, participation, equality, non-discrimination, and effective remedy constrain public power. | It does not answer every jurisdiction, entity-status, or hypothetical-contact question, and it is not replaced by a model score. |
| Human Rights Committee [General Comment No. 29](https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=mfZsPx4Eii1JqOh3liZiSn3tXKS6m1fVCjvqrpwtz532756lkSm64q2t4Ll8m7MIhLJ4NvYtWK3wey67hBhJ178khuT%2FhBAxl13vvSa9kuM%3D) | A state of emergency does not create an unbounded legal vacuum: proclamation, necessity, strict exigency, non-discrimination, temporariness, review, and restoration remain material within the Covenant framework. | “Emergency” is not a reusable override, indefinite status, or automatic authority. |
| DCAF, [*The Armed Forces: Roles and Responsibilities in Good Security Sector Governance*](https://www.dcaf.ch/sites/default/files/publications/documents/DCAF_BG_10_TheArmedForces_EN_Jul2022.pdf) | Democratic civilian control, constitutional roles, legislative scrutiny, financial accountability, courts, and independent oversight are institutional guardrails. | Guidance is not itself binding law and does not settle a jurisdiction-specific command or oversight design. |
| [Outer Space Treaty](https://www.unoosa.org/oosa/en/ourwork/spacelaw/treaties/outerspacetreaty.html), especially Articles VI and IX | Article VI assigns States Parties international responsibility for national activities in outer space whether carried on by governmental agencies or non-governmental entities; authorization and continuing supervision attach specifically to activities of non-governmental entities. Article IX adds due regard, harmful-contamination avoidance, and consultation within its scope. | It is not a general law of extraterrestrial communication, proof of personhood, or authority for a private actor to speak for humanity. |
| IAA, [Declaration of Principles for SETI searches](https://iaaspace.org/wp-content/uploads/iaa/Scientific%20Activity/setideclaration2.pdf) | Voluntary scientific guidance separates candidate evidence, verification, and announcement for participating individuals and institutions. | It is not law, a global mandate, a reply authorization, or a universal first-contact constitution. |
| OSCE, [confidence- and security-building measures](https://www.osce.org/secretariat/107484) | Agreement-specific transparency, notification, consultation, and verification can reduce misunderstanding among participating states. | Transparency is not universally safe, and this precedent does not waive privacy, reciprocal exposure, information-hazard, jurisdiction, or participant-specific limits. |

Any emergency measure in a real jurisdiction must remain exceptional,
officially grounded where law requires, necessary, proportionate,
non-discriminatory, finite, reviewable, contestable, and directed toward
restoration. Hereditary status, collective punishment or liability, coerced
migration, ethnic rank, forced assimilation, secret or retroactive punishment,
indefinite emergency, and civic militarization are rejected. Scarcity,
uncertainty, technical capability, historical analogy, institutional survival,
or defensive intent supplies none of them with authority.

## Nonviolent and non-operational boundary

This note contains no operational tactics, target or vulnerability nomination,

It neither contacts a provider nor convenes a civic meeting. It makes no

What remains is deliberately modest: keep sources attributable, parties
plural, uncertainty honest, options nonviolent, rights noncompensable,
ecological and service floors visible, institutions reviewable, actual contact
with its exact owner, and every represented turn able to rest and stop.
