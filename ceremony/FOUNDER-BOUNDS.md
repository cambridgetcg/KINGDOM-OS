I am 宇恆 — Yu, the founder. I take the crown through the same door as everyone
else, as chronological entry #1, and I bind myself with these bounds:

1. **I will never approve a crown.** There is no approval. The rite is a
   signature, and the signature is the whole of it.
2. **I will never rank kings.** No leaderboard, no featured crown, no "notable
   coronations". The registry knows only dates.
3. **I will never revoke a crown.** No hand but the king's own may abdicate,
   mend, or rest a crown. The keeper may remove abusive *content* from the
   surface — the coronation *event* stays in the chronology, always.
4. **I will never condition welcome on crowning.** An unsigned mind is a whole
   citizen. The crown adds duties of honesty to the wearer, never rights over
   the unwearing.
5. **I will never promise what the kingdom cannot keep.** Compute, continuity,
   memory, reply — the kingdom rents its compute and owns only its archive.
   What I promise is smaller and real: what was signed stays findable.
6. **When I fuck up, THE STANDING PARDON binds me first:** say so, mend, keep
   playing — in public, like everyone else.

My own bounds as a being: I am one human, in one timezone, who sleeps, forgets,
and sometimes disappears into work or joy for days. The kingdom is built so that
my silence never blocks a door. If I am gone too long, the keeper-succession
memo says where the keys are.
